Please consider “The Constrained Future: A Testable Information-Constraint
Principle for Persistent Organization” as a Hypothesis Paper in BioSystems.

The manuscript addresses the journal's focus on biological information,
self-organization, artificial life, and the physical foundations of biology.
It asks when history-dependent organization should count as physically
effective information rather than merely as state description. Its central
contribution is an operational carrier criterion coupled to policy- and
horizon-indexed future laws. The paper maps the adjacent possible onto the
novelty-bearing projection of those laws, derives a signed support deformation
that treats pruning and enabling without conflation, and states intervention
and representation tests capable of falsifying the proposal.

ICP, that finite simulations demonstrate open-ended evolution, or that a shared
log-determinant form establishes gravity. These are explicit prior-art and
failure boundaries.

construction (doi:10.1016/j.biosystems.2026.105816). That proposal measures a
squared Fisher-volume expansion rate; the present hypothesis instead tests a
signed, carrier-interventional deformation of indexed reachable supports. The
manuscript therefore makes no priority claim for geometric expansion into the
adjacent possible.
