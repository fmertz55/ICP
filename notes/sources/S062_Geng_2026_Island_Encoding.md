## Native result

For standard island models and explicitly the Karch–Randall braneworld, Geng
argues that bath coupling Higgses gravitational diffeomorphisms. The associated
Goldstone/Stückelberg dressing lets bath generators act on dressed bulk island
operators, providing a local account of their otherwise nonlocal encoding.

## Common-schema mapping

Here the candidate carrier is not “the Page curve” but a specified broken-
symmetry/dressing structure linking bath operations to island observables.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

This is the strongest mechanism-level ICP analogue in the batch, but its scope
is massive-gravity/open-system island models. It does not establish that the
same carrier exists in standard asymptotically flat gravity.
