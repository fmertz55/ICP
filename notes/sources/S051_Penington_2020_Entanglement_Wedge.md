# S051 — Penington (2020), Entanglement-Wedge Reconstruction

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `Penington2020EntanglementWedge`

- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article
- Stratum: evaporating AdS black hole and Page transition

## Native result

For an AdS/CFT black hole with absorbing boundary conditions, a quantum
RT-surface transition occurs at the Page time. After the transition, part of
the interior lies in the radiation entanglement wedge, yielding a Page curve
and a Hayden–Preskill reconstruction criterion without a firewall.

## Common-schema mapping

The encoding changes which logical interior operators are reconstructable from
the collected radiation. Knowledge of the initial state and code-subspace size
affect when a state-independent reconstruction exists.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

This is a controlled holographic/bath result, not a complete microscopic model
of an asymptotically flat astrophysical evaporation. Reconstructability does
not imply efficient decodability.
