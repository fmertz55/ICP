# S070 — Lieb and Yngvason (2013), Entropy for Non-equilibrium States

## Bibliographic record

- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.2013.0408>
- Primary manuscript: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.3912>
- Status: peer-reviewed axiomatic theoretical article

## Native result

For generic nonequilibrium states, a unique entropy with all desired physical
properties generally does not exist. Quantities `S_-` and `S_+` bound which
equilibrium states can reach or be reached from the nonequilibrium state.

## Common-schema mapping

Incomparability and interval-valued accessibility information can be more
fundamental than a scalar summary.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The result uses equilibrium comparison states and adiabatic accessibility. It
does not provide a universal nonequilibrium state function.
