## Why included

Empirical anchor for the criticality candidate rather than relying only on a
formal energy-entropy identity.

## Native result

A maximum-entropy model fitted to retinal neural activity is examined across
population sizes and an effective-temperature deformation. The authors report
a growing heat-capacity peak and related signatures interpreted as approach to
criticality.
