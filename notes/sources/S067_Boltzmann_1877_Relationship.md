# S067 — Boltzmann (1877), Relationship between the Second Law and Probability

## Bibliographic record

- English translation: <https://doi.org/10.3390/e17041971>
- Status: foundational historical article; peer-reviewed 2015 translation

## Native result

Boltzmann develops a combinatorial measure of the multiplicity of microscopic
arrangements compatible with a macroscopic occupation description.

## Common-schema mapping

The macro-partition, conserved totals, counting measure, and boundary define the
compatible set before its logarithmic multiplicity is evaluated.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

Equal multiplicity does not imply equal state-space geometry, barriers,
connectivity, or transition laws. The construction does not make microscopic
entropy monotonically increase.
