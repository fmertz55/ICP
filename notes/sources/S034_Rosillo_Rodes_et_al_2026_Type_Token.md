## Why included

Current correction to mechanical exponent conversion between inverse-power
rankings and type-token growth.

## Native result

For an idealized deterministic growing system maintaining a count ranking
`S proportional to r^(-a)`, the authors derive a unified asymptotic
type-token expression. For `a < 1`, type growth is asymptotically linear; at
`a = 1`, logarithmic corrections dominate; for large `a`, the familiar
inverse-exponent relation emerges.

## Common-schema mapping

The work shows what follows from a specified growing rank construction. It does
not supply arbitrary token order, history equivalence, or a carrier.

## Limitations and rival reading

This is not a universal sampling theorem. Applying `beta = 1/a` near one or to
an unrelated observed sequence is outside the exact result.
