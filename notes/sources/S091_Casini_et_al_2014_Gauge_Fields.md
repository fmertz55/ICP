# S091 — Casini, Huerta, and Rosabal (2014), Entanglement Entropy for Gauge Fields

## Bibliographic record

- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.89.085012>
- Primary manuscript: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.1183>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article

## Native result

Gauge constraints obstruct naive tensor factorization. Regional algebras can
have centers, and electric, magnetic, or trivial-center choices yield different
entropy assignments; mutual information and relative entropy are more robust
in the studied continuum limits.

## Common-schema mapping

The entropy belongs to a declared observable algebra, not to an unqualified
geometric region.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

Algebra choice can create apparent entropy differences with no change to the
global state. ICP must distinguish access from bookkeeping.
