## Native result

Topological entropy measures asymptotic orbit complexity through refinements of
open covers; for symbolic systems it tracks growth of admissible words.

## Common-schema mapping

Hard constraints can remove orbit segments and reduce support growth without
specifying which remaining paths are probable.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The topology, coding, compact state space, and resolution are part of the
object. Equal topological entropy can conceal different continuation languages.
