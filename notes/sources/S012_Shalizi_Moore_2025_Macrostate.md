## Why included

Connects computational-mechanics prediction to emergent macrostates while
making observer and coarse-graining dependence explicit.

## Native result and formalism

Starting from chosen observables of Markov microdynamics, causal-state
refinement constructs macrostates sufficient for predicting those observables.
Once the observation map is fixed, objective dynamics fixes the refinement.

## Common-schema mapping

- `H`: history of the selected coarse observable.
- `M`: its causal-state macrostate.
- `Pi`: observation and choice of predictive task.
- Future object: future values of the chosen macro-observables.
- Claim type: predictive emergence.

## Assumptions, limitations, and rival reading

The microstate is assumed Markovian, while measurement map, temporal scale, and
resolution are chosen. No unique global macrodescription follows. Appendix B's
position/velocity example shows that state augmentation can remove apparent
memory.
