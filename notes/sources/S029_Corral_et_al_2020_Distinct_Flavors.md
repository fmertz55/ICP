# S029 — Corral, Serra, and Ferrer-i-Cancho (2020), Distinct Flavors of Zipf's Law

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `CorralEtAl2020DistinctFlavors`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.102.052113>
- Status: peer-reviewed statistical methods article
- Stratum: Zipf representations and finite discrete inference

## Why included

Prevents silent switching between rank-size and size-distribution formulations
after seeing which one looks better.

## Native result

Mathematical analysis and simulations show that a pure discrete power law in
rank does not yield an exact power law in the count PMF, and vice versa. MLE on
empirical ranks can reject data constructed to be Zipfian, while fitting the
size/count distribution more reliably recovers its generating exponent.

## Common-schema mapping

The estimand and observable algebra are part of boundary `B`. A mechanism must
specify whether it generates ranks, counts, token paths, or all three.

## Limitations and rival reading

Asymptotic exponent conversions remain useful under stated conditions. The
paper does not identify a causal mechanism.
