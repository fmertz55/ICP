# S039 — Hawking (1976), Breakdown of Predictability

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `Hawking1976Breakdown`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.14.2460>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article
- Stratum: original black-hole information-loss argument

## Native result

Hawking argues that complete evaporation maps an initially pure state to a
mixed final density matrix. He therefore replaces an ordinary unitary
S-matrix with a superscattering map from density matrices to density matrices.

## Common-schema mapping

The hidden interior/singularity is traced over, so the asymptotic description
loses distinctions among formation histories. The conclusion depends on the
semiclassical spacetime, the chosen inside/outside separation, and the assumed
algebra of final measurements.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

This is the paradox-generating result, not evidence that information is a new
physical constraint. Later work contests its factorization and completeness
assumptions. It is direct evidence that the algebra and system boundary cannot
be left implicit.
