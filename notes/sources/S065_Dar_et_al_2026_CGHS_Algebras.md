## Native result

Under the usual holography-of-information assumptions, the authors construct
the radiative phase space and future-null-infinity algebras of the CGHS model
with a massless scalar. They recover the state of right-moving modes from an
arbitrarily small neighborhood of right future null infinity and argue that an
island conflicts with commuting left/right boundary algebras.

## Common-schema mapping

The result makes the algebra and left/right factorization assumption explicit;
the apparent conflict is therefore directly indexed by `A` rather than by an
unqualified scalar “amount of information.”

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The scope is two-dimensional CGHS/Fock quantization and does not include a
finite black-hole microstate entropy or a global RST replica-wormhole
derivation. It sharpens but does not settle the current dispute.
