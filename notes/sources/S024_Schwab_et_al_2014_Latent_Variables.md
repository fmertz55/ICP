# S024 — Schwab, Nemenman, and Mehta (2014), Zipf and Criticality without Fine-Tuning

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `SchwabEtAl2014LatentVariables`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.113.068102>
- Status: peer-reviewed theory and simulation
- Stratum: Zipf mechanisms; latent mixtures and criticality

## Why included

Strongest general rival to the inference that a Zipf state distribution shows
an intrinsically tuned critical or history-bearing system.

## Native result

When a broad unobserved variable coherently modulates many degrees of freedom,
mixing conditional distributions over that variable generically produces a
Zipf-like multivariate state distribution in large systems. Fine tuning to one
intrinsic critical point is unnecessary.

## Common-schema mapping

- `H`: pooled observations under changing hidden condition.
- `M`: current latent input, if measured; otherwise omitted.
- Future law: narrow conditional state law mixed into a broad marginal.
- ICP status: confound unless the latent condition is itself a persistent,
  embodied, manipulable record.

## Limitations and rival reading

Broad coherent modulation is required, and some latent transformations do not
produce Zipf. The construction is a causal alternative, not proof that every
observed Zipf law is a mixture.
