# S080 — Chetrite and Touchette (2015), Markov Processes Conditioned on Large Deviations

## Bibliographic record

- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00023-014-0375-8>
- Primary manuscript: <https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.5157>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article

## Native result

A tilted generator and generalized Doob transform construct a driven process
asymptotically equivalent to a rare-event-conditioned Markov process under
stated spectral, convexity, and ensemble-equivalence conditions.

## Common-schema mapping

Conditioning selects path sectors and can make rare behavior typical under a
new effective law.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

Postselection is not a surgical intervention, and logarithmic long-time
equivalence is not exact finite-time equality.
