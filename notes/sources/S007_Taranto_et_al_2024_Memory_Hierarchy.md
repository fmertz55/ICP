## Why included

Gives a carrier-sensitive taxonomy rather than collapsing all temporal
correlation into one kind of memory.

## Native result and formalism

For three or more times the authors distinguish Markovian, mixed-memory,
classical-memory, separable, and fully quantum-memory process classes. Classical
memory is physically modeled through measure-and-prepare or
entanglement-breaking environmental channels.

## Common-schema mapping

- `H`: earlier multitime operations and outcomes.
- `M`: an environmental classical outcome register or coherent quantum state.
- `Pi`: conditional instruments at later times.
- Future object: probabilities generated by the multitime process Choi state.
- Claim type: interventional-operational carrier classification.

## Assumptions, limitations, and rival reading

Non-Markovianity does not imply quantum memory. Choi-state separability does not
by itself establish a realizable classical carrier, and two-time observations
cannot distinguish the full hierarchy.
