- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article

## Native result

For specified catalytic thermal operations on energy-incoherent states, a
family of Rényi-divergence generalized free energies constrains and, under the
paper's assumptions, characterizes state conversion.

## Common-schema mapping

Hamiltonian, Gibbs reference, bath, catalyst return condition, and error regime
define the reachable-state preorder before its monotones are evaluated.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

One ordinary free energy is insufficient. Coherence and permissive approximate
catalysis require additional care.
