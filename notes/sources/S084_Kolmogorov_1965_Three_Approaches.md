## Native result

Kolmogorov develops an individual-object information quantity based on the
length of a shortest effective description.

## Common-schema mapping

Regular generative rules can shorten the description of one history without an
ensemble probability model.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

Exact Kolmogorov complexity is uncomputable; reference-machine dependence is
only asymptotically bounded. Compressor output is an upper-bound proxy, not
thermodynamic entropy, prediction memory, or runtime.
