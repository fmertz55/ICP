## Native result

The authors argue that islands exist with massless gravitons and without an
external bath, including boundary-CFT subregions and proper radiation
subalgebras at null infinity. Around generic backgrounds without isometries,
they construct compactly supported gauge-invariant operators order by order in
perturbation theory and propose a many-copy Page-curve measurement.

## Common-schema mapping

Their Page curve is indexed by a proper radiation algebra that excludes the
larger asymptotic algebra containing the ADM Hamiltonian. Reconstruction is
perturbative, code-subspace-relative, and potentially exponentially complex.
