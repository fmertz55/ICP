# S073 — Seifert (2005), Entropy Production along a Stochastic Trajectory

## Bibliographic record

- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.95.040602>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article

## Native result

Seifert defines system-plus-medium entropy production for individual stochastic
trajectories and derives an integral fluctuation theorem.

## Common-schema mapping

Transition rates, the bath model, path resolution, and initial distribution
define the quantity. Coarse-graining hidden states can change inferred
production.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

Trajectory entropy production is time-reversal asymmetry in a modeled path
law; it is not automatically a carrier or organization measure.
