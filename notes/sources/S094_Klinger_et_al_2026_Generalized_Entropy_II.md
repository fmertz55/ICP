# S094 — Klinger, Kudler-Flam, and Satishchandran (2026), Generalized Entropy II

## Bibliographic record

- Primary preprint: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.07910>
- Version used: v1, 12 January 2026
- Status: non-peer-reviewed theoretical preprint

## Native result

For perturbative stationary-horizon and de Sitter settings, angle-dependent
horizon charges and edge modes add an infinite constraint family to the
physical observable algebra. The authors construct a Type-II algebra whose
entropy includes generalized entropy, an edge term, and a constant.

## Common-schema mapping

Completing the symmetry and charge algebra changes both accessible distinctions
and the entropy assigned to a semiclassical state.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The result is perturbative, model-specific, and unreviewed. It does not establish
a nonperturbative evaporation channel or a universal constraint principle.
