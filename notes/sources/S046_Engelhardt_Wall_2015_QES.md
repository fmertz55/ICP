# S046 — Engelhardt and Wall (2015), Quantum Extremal Surfaces

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `EngelhardtWall2015QES`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP01(2015)073>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article
- Stratum: generalized entropy and quantum extremal surfaces

## Native result

The paper proposes that holographic entanglement entropy beyond the classical
regime is computed by a surface extremizing generalized entropy: area divided
by `4 G_N` plus bulk entanglement entropy. It studies causal properties and
barriers of such quantum extremal surfaces.

## Common-schema mapping

A QES selects an entropy/reconstruction boundary relative to a specified
boundary region and semiclassical expansion. It is neither a worldline of
information nor a set of counterfactual future trajectories.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The prescription is foundational for island calculations, but its native
content is an entropy extremization rule in holographic gravity. ICP must not
relabel generalized entropy as a generic “constraint” measure.
