# S076 — Sinai (1959), On the Concept of Entropy for a Dynamical System

## Bibliographic record

- English translation: <https://nikolaivivanov.files.wordpress.com/2015/05/definitionentropy2014-20151.pdf>
- Status: foundational peer-reviewed mathematical article

## Native result

Sinai shows that a generating partition can be used to calculate the entropy
of a measure-preserving dynamical system.

## Common-schema mapping

An observation alphabet captures the dynamical distinctions only when its
histories distinguish the relevant states; otherwise its entropy is a
coarse-grained projection.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

Generating status, invariant measure, and measure preservation must be shown.
Entropy that keeps changing under justified refinement is not a complete
constraint measure.
