# S056 — Raju (2022), Failure of the Split Property

## Bibliographic record

## Native result

Raju argues that gravity lacks the ordinary QFT split property: sufficiently
rich observables near the boundary determine the state on the full Cauchy
slice. On this account, the factorization used by Hawking, AMPS, and a naive
full-exterior Page curve is not valid in standard gravity.

## Common-schema mapping

Changing from a restricted radiation algebra to a complete asymptotic algebra
can change the entropy and “information emergence” story while leaving the
global state unchanged.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

This is highly relevant to ICP's `A` index, but it is an active position rather
than consensus. The operational precision, time, and algebra needed for
boundary completeness must be declared.
