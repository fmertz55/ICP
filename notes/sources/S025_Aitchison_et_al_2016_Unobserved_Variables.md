# S025 — Aitchison, Corradi, and Latham (2016), Underlying Unobserved Variables

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `AitchisonEtAl2016UnderlyingVariables`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1005110>
- Status: peer-reviewed theory and empirical tests
- Stratum: Zipf mechanisms; latent conditioning

## Why included

Turns the latent-mixture alternative into empirical diagnostics rather than a
generic verbal objection.

## Native result

The paper studies when unobserved-variable mixtures generate Zipf and defines
the proportion of energy variance explained (PEEV) by a candidate latent
variable. In the linguistic application, pooled word frequencies are Zipfian
while most part-of-speech strata are not; the reported PEEV is substantial.

## Common-schema mapping

- `M`: candidate latent state or proxy.
- `pi`: conditioning or experimental narrowing of the latent input.
- Future law: conditional versus pooled state distribution.
- ICP status: mandatory confound test before intrinsic or inherited claims.

## Limitations and rival reading

Conditioning can be incomplete, proxies can be noisy, and residual scaling does
not select a single alternative. A latent label is not automatically a carrier.
