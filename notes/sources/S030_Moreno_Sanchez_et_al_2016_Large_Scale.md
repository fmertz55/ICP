# S030 — Moreno-Sánchez, Font-Clos, and Corral (2016), Large-Scale Zipf Analysis

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `MorenoSanchezEtAl2016LargeScale`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0147073>
- Status: peer-reviewed large-corpus empirical analysis
- Stratum: Zipf inference and empirical scope

## Why included

Tests multiple Zipf formulations across 31,075 English Project Gutenberg texts
rather than relying on a few canonical examples.

## Native result

Three discrete formulations are fitted with likelihood and Monte Carlo KS
testing. The best one-parameter whole-domain complementary-cumulative form is
compatible at `p >= 0.05` with only about 40 percent of texts. Rank is treated
as a problematic post-sample random variable; type counts are the primary
inferential object.

## Common-schema mapping

This bounds claims about empirical universality. It supplies no physical
mechanism or carrier.

## Limitations and rival reading

The analysis approximates type counts as independent and uses a limited number
of Monte Carlo replicates. The 40 percent figure is corpus- and formulation-
specific, not a language constant.
