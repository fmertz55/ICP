# S032 — Gerlach and Altmann (2013), Stochastic Vocabulary Growth

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `GerlachAltmann2013VocabularyGrowth`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevX.3.021006>
- Status: peer-reviewed model and large-corpus analysis
- Stratum: Zipf mechanisms; vocabulary growth

## Why included

Provides a richer growth alternative to a constant-innovation Simon process and
links two rank regimes to vocabulary dynamics.

## Native result

The model separates a finite high-frequency core vocabulary from a virtually
unbounded noncore vocabulary whose reuse reduces future novelty probability.
It fits two scaling regimes in Google Ngram data and describes historical
turnover of core words.

## Common-schema mapping

- `H`: prior word introductions and reuse.
- `M`: core/noncore membership and accumulated counts.
- Future law: new-word probability and reuse distribution.
- ICP status: history-sensitive stochastic candidate without a physical carrier.

## Limitations and rival reading

Corpus filtering and aggregation affect the inferred regimes. The model is a
descriptive stochastic mechanism, not a unique account of language production.
