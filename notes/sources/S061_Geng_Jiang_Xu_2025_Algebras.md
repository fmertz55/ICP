# S061 — Geng, Jiang, and Xu (2025), Algebras, Islands, and Observers

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `GengJiangXu2025Algebras`
- Stable URL: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12127>
- Status: non-peer-reviewed preprint, version 1
- Stratum: observer-dressed island algebras

## Native result

In an AdS gravitational system coupled to a nongravitating bath, the authors
construct the island “observer” from the Goldstone vector of spontaneously
broken diffeomorphism symmetry. They argue that consistent island operators
must be dressed to this observer and that the corresponding holographic
algebra is type II-infinity.

## Common-schema mapping

The bath controls both the observer dynamics and the algebra relative to which
reconstruction is defined. The work makes observer/algebra dependence a
physical part of the model rather than a semantic label.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The result assumes the geometric modular-flow conjecture and an island model
with an external bath. It does not establish an observer-independent algebra
for standard massless gravity.
