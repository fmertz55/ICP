# S023 — Ferrer i Cancho and Solé (2003), Least Effort and the Origins of Scaling

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `FerrerCanchoSole2003LeastEffort`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0335980100>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical and simulation article
- Stratum: Zipf mechanisms; communicative optimization

## Why included

Supplies a non-growth mechanism with predictions tying frequency scaling to
ambiguity and communicative information.

## Native result

A binary signal-referent association matrix minimizes a weighted combination
of hearer ambiguity and speaker entropy. Near a transition around
`lambda approximately 0.41`, the signal-frequency distribution is Zipf-like
while mutual information and effective lexicon size change sharply.

## Common-schema mapping

- `M`: optimized association matrix.
- `pi`: referent and communicative-cost conditions.
- Future law: conditional signal choice.
- ICP status: candidate organized code only if the matrix is learned, embodied,
  and manipulable.

## Limitations and rival reading

Uniform referents, binary associations, heuristic optimization, and a tuned
transition limit direct application. The model does not explain physical
formation or storage of the code.
