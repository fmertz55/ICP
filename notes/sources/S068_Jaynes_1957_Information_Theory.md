# S068 — Jaynes (1957), Information Theory and Statistical Mechanics

## Bibliographic record

- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.106.620>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article

## Native result

Jaynes derives statistical-mechanical ensembles by maximizing Shannon entropy
over distributions satisfying specified expectation constraints.

## Common-schema mapping

The constraints define an inferential feasible set. Lagrange multipliers become
physical thermodynamic quantities only when the observables and preparation
are independently tied to exchange, conservation, or response.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The same Gibbs-shaped distribution can arise from equilibrium, driven
preparation, marginalization, or limited-information inference. Form alone does
not identify an embodied constraint.
