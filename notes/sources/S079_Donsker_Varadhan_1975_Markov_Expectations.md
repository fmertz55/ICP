# S079 — Donsker and Varadhan (1975), Markov Process Expectations

## Bibliographic record

- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1002/cpa.3160280102>
- Status: peer-reviewed mathematical article

## Native result

The paper develops long-time asymptotics and rate-function machinery for
functionals of Markov processes.

## Common-schema mapping

A large-deviation rate function records how exponentially costly different
future sectors are, retaining tail structure omitted by an entropy mean.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

Long-time scaling, Markov assumptions, and regularity conditions are essential.
A rate function is not an entropy rate or a physical intervention.
