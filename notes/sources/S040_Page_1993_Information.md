# S040 — Page (1993), Information in Black Hole Radiation

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `Page1993Information`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.71.3743>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article
- Stratum: unitary random-state Page curve

## Native result

Assuming black-hole formation and evaporation admit a unitary S-matrix and
that the joint state behaves typically, Page estimates that information is
initially almost absent from the radiation and emerges around the entropy
halfway point. Perturbative calculations may therefore fail to see it.

## Common-schema mapping

The result concerns the entropy and information of a selected radiation
subsystem under typical-state assumptions. It does not identify the
microscopic transfer mechanism, a local carrier, or an efficient decoder.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The Page curve is a conditional benchmark for unitarity, not a measurement of
counterfactual future states. It is one functional of a reduced state after a
factorization has been declared.
