## Native result

Under stated Euclidean-path-integral axioms for a UV completion of
asymptotically AdS gravity, the construction yields type-I von Neumann boundary
algebras that are mutual commutants, Hilbert spaces, and entropies. In suitable
semiclassical limits the entropies reproduce quantum-corrected RT/HRT.

## Common-schema mapping

Entropy is explicitly an entropy of an algebra of observables. This is a
cleaner object for ICP than the unqualified phrase “information in a region.”

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The theorem rests on path-integral axioms, AdS asymptotics, compact boundary
components, and positivity. Plausible extension to other UV completions is not
a demonstrated universal result.
