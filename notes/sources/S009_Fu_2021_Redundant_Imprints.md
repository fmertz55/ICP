# S009 — Fu (2021), Redundant Imprints and Environment Partitions

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `Fu2021RedundantImprints`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.103.042210>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article
- Stratum: quantum Darwinism boundary

## Why included

Tests whether a supposedly objective environmental record is invariant under
how the environment is decomposed.

## Native result and formalism

The same global quantum state can support redundant imprints of noncommuting
observables under different decompositions of the environment. A uniqueness
result is recovered only for appropriately comparable partitions, using an
extended tridecomposition/semi-GHZ structure.

## Common-schema mapping

- `H`: prior system-environment interaction, implicit in the global state.
- `M`: records carried by selected environment fragments.
- `Pi`: local fragment measurements.
- Future object: present accessibility and agreement, not a controlled future
  process.
- Claim type: predictive-observational accessibility.

## Assumptions, limitations, and rival reading

Redundancy depends on a tensor factorization and locality/comparability
conditions. A redundant record need not causally change later dynamics, while a
process memory need not be redundantly observable.
