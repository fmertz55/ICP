## Native result

In a two-boundary JT-gravity/CFT model, coupling one side to an external
auxiliary system produces evaporation. Large gradients of bulk entropy create
a new QES, a Page-time transition, and scrambling-time behavior consistent
with a holographic Hayden–Preskill protocol.

## Common-schema mapping

The auxiliary coupling is a genuine intervention changing the system boundary
and the reconstructable algebra. Generalized entropy determines a QES in the
specified semiclassical model.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The result is model-specific and assumes a nongravitating heat sink. It does
not establish the mechanism for a four-dimensional isolated black hole.
