# S017 — Marletto (2016), Constructor Theory of Probability

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `Marletto2016Probability`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.2015.0883>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article
- Stratum: constructor theory; probability boundary

## Why included

Tests whether constructor theory already supplies the probability layer needed
for ICP's future ensembles.

## Native result and formalism

The paper separates unpredictability from stochasticity, derives
unpredictability from non-clonability in superinformation theories, and gives
sufficient conditions under which partitions of unity support rational
decisions as if they were probabilities.

## Common-schema mapping

- `H`: not a primitive object.
- `M`: a superinformation attribute used in a measurement game.
- `Pi`: measurement and decision-game operations.
- Future object: alternative outcomes with decision-supporting numerical
  weights.
- Claim type: decision-theoretic; not an objective path law.

## Assumptions, limitations, and rival reading

The result requires a decision-supporting superinformation theory with
substantial physical, symmetry, measurement, and rationality conditions. The
paper explicitly says the result is not a derivation of the Born rule and does
not conclude that the weights are probabilities.

## ICP relationship

- Direction/directness: qualifies / direct.
- Break point: "as if probability" for decisions is not `P^pi(F | M)`, an
  empirical frequency law, or a structural counterfactual distribution.
