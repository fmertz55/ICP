# S053 — Penington, Shenker, Stanford, and Yang (2022), Replica Wormholes

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `PeningtonEtAl2022ReplicaWormholes`

- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article; arXiv 2019
- Stratum: replica trick, Page transition, and Petz recovery

## Native result

The paper obtains the Page transition by summing replica geometries with
different topologies in controlled models, including simple gravity/matrix
settings, JT gravity with matter, and SYK-related examples. It also gives a
gravitational Petz-map argument for entanglement-wedge reconstruction.

## Common-schema mapping

Replica wormholes calculate nonlinear entropy/recovery quantities across
replicas. The Petz map states an in-principle recovery channel on a code, not an
efficient laboratory decoder.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The authors explicitly discuss an ensemble-average interpretation. A Euclidean
replica saddle is not automatically a Lorentzian carrier or microscopic
transfer process.
