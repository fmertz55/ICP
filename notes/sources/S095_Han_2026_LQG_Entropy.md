## Native result

For fixed-area loop-quantum-gravity states on a graph, a nonfactor Type-I
operator algebra and maximum-entropy construction yield a leading geometrical
area law with bulk renormalization and logarithmic corrections.

## Common-schema mapping

Gauge invariance, the area spectrum, intertwiner sectors, and admissibility
conditions select the state sectors before entropy is maximized.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The result is a graph-based fixed-area calculation, not a universal dynamical
derivation of black-hole entropy. Different constraint schemes can share the
leading area term while differing subleadingly.
