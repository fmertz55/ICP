# S049 — Hawking, Perry, and Strominger (2016), Soft Hair

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `HawkingPerryStrominger2016SoftHair`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.116.231301>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article
- Stratum: asymptotic symmetries and soft charges

## Native result

BMS supertranslations and gauge symmetry imply infinitely many conserved soft
gravitational or electromagnetic charges. The paper describes horizon soft
hair and exact relations between evaporation products for black holes that
differ in their soft state.

## Common-schema mapping

Soft charges are candidate physical records of distinctions among histories,
and charge-sensitive asymptotic measurements form the observable algebra.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

An infinite charge family does not show that generic infalling quantum
information is encoded with sufficient capacity or is recoverable. The result
is a candidate mechanism, not a complete resolution of the paradox.
