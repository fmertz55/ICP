# S005 — Pollock et al. (2018), Operational Markov Condition

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `PollockEtAl2018Markov`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.120.040405>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article
- Stratum: quantum memory and process tensors

## Why included

Supplies a concrete intervention—causal break—for asking whether information
from an earlier history still has operational consequences.

## Native result and formalism

A causal break measures the system, discards its state, and reprepares it. A
process is operationally Markovian exactly when the future after every such
break is independent of earlier controls and outcomes conditional on the newly
prepared state.

## Common-schema mapping

- `H`: controls and outcomes before the break.
- `M`: memory outside the overwritten system that can cross the break.
- `Pi`: causal-break and later instruments.
- Future object: conditional output states and measurement distributions.
- Claim type: intervention-relative operational independence.

## Assumptions, limitations, and rival reading

The test depends on the selected system, temporal resolution, and accessible
operation set. It detects operational influence but does not uniquely identify
the carrier or define an observer-independent amount of memory.

## ICP relationship

- Direction/directness: supports and qualifies / direct.
- Experimental lesson: reset the declared present state, then ask whether
  controlled historical differences still change future statistics.
- Break point: failure of Markovity in a reduced process is not fundamental
  historical inheritance.
