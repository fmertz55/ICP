# S033 — De Marzo et al. (2021), Dynamical Approach to Zipf's Law

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `DeMarzoEtAl2021DynamicalZipf`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.3.013084>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical and empirical article
- Stratum: longitudinal Zipf dynamics

## Why included

Distinguishes systems dynamically attracted toward Zipf from snapshots that
look Zipfian only temporarily through finite sampling.

## Native result

For a truncated power-law size distribution the paper relates deviation from
Zipf to the number of elements and lower/upper support cutoffs. A coherence
condition tracks whether support evolution keeps pace with population growth.
Applications distinguish coherent and spurious cases and use the distinction
for model selection and extreme-size estimates.

## Common-schema mapping

- `H`: system-growth trajectory.
- `M`: current cutoffs, exponent, and population size.
- Future law: longitudinal evolution of ranks and support.
- ICP status: diagnostic of co-evolving possibility space, not a unique carrier.

## Limitations and rival reading

The diagnostic assumes a truncated power-law PDF and depends on cutoff and time
definitions. Several causal mechanisms can satisfy coherence.
