# S092 — Wilming, Gallego, and Eisert (2016), Second Law under Control Restrictions

## Bibliographic record

- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.93.042126>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article

## Native result

The paper derives work-extraction bounds when only a restricted family of
Hamiltonians and controls is available.

## Common-schema mapping

Holding the state and thermodynamic environment fixed while changing the
controller changes the operational future set and obtainable work.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The bound is explicitly control-relative. It is strong native prior art for an
ICP experiment at matched state entropy and a strong redundancy test.
