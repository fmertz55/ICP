# S075 — Kolmogorov (1958), A New Metric Invariant

## Bibliographic record

- Primary record: <https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/dan22922>
- Status: foundational peer-reviewed mathematical article

## Native result

Kolmogorov introduces measure-theoretic entropy for measure-preserving
dynamical systems from joined observation partitions and their asymptotic
information rate.

## Common-schema mapping

The dynamics, invariant measure, and observation partition jointly determine
the information-production rate.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

Metric entropy is neither topological path growth nor thermodynamic entropy.
Zero metric entropy does not imply absence of organized or long-memory dynamics.
