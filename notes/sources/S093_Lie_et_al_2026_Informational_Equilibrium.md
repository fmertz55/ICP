# S093 — Lie et al. (2026), Thermal Operations from Informational Equilibrium

- Published: 13 July 2026, Physical Review Letters 137, 030403
- Status: peer-reviewed current theoretical article

## Native result

Thermal operations are characterized by a dilation property called
informational equilibrium: when the system is prepared in equilibrium, the
environment is left invariant. Requiring invariance for all inputs yields
catalytic channels, and the paper derives a strict channel hierarchy.

## Common-schema mapping

The result characterizes an operation class at the channel-environment level;
the Gibbs description is represented downstream of that structure.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The characterization depends on the declared dilation and equilibrium
conditions. It is not a universal entropy law or empirical validation of ICP.
