- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.85.085004>

## Native result

Using an extended Hilbert-space construction, lattice-gauge entanglement
decomposes into sector Shannon entropy, representation-dimension terms, and
within-sector correlations.

## Common-schema mapping

Gauss constraints and boundary representation sectors alter what regional
degrees of freedom and entropy contributions are available.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

themselves evidence of a history-bearing carrier.
