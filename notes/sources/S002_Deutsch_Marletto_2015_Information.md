# S002 — Deutsch and Marletto (2015), Constructor Theory of Information

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `DeutschMarletto2015Information`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1098/rspa.2014.0540>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article
- Stratum: constructor theory of information

## Why included

Gives the constructor-theoretic definition of information in explicitly
physical task terms and is therefore central to any ICP use of "embodied
information."

## Native result and formalism

The paper characterizes computation and information variables through possible
permutation, copying, and distinguishability tasks. Information is not assumed
to be an abstract primitive; its properties are tied to the laws governing
physical media.

## Common-schema mapping

- `H`: absent from the base information-variable definition.
- `M`: an information medium instantiating an information variable.
- `Pi`: copying, permutation, measurement, and distinguishing tasks.
- Future object: possible transformations of substrate attributes.
- Claim type: modal-operational, not a probability forecast.

## Assumptions, limitations, and rival reading

The framework includes conjectured Principles III–VIII and assumes broad
storage and processing resources. The paper explicitly sets aside information's
long-term future. It establishes a physical task repertoire, not that a realized
historical record causes a particular finite-time future distribution.

## ICP relationship

- Direction/directness: supports / direct for a physical embodiment criterion.
- What ICP must add: formation, persistence, and intervention on the record,
  plus an explicit future-law object.
- Break point: copying and distinguishability do not alone imply inherited
  constraint or cross-world counterfactuals.
