- Citation key: `MoraBialek2011Criticality`

- Status: peer-reviewed review and theoretical synthesis
- Stratum: Zipf mechanisms; criticality

## Why included

Defines the criticality interpretation and, importantly, the additional
finite-size and response signatures it should imply beyond a rank slope.

## Native result

Writing `E(x) = -log P(x)`, a Zipf rank law corresponds to a near-linear
microcanonical entropy-energy relation. The review relates this statistical
identity to broad fluctuations and critical-like organization and discusses
finite-size scaling and biological examples.

## Common-schema mapping

- `M`: fitted interactions and operating point.

- Future law: response and fluctuation behavior in the model.
