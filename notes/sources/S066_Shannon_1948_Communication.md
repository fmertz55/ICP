## Native result

Shannon defines entropy for a probability distribution and develops source
entropy rates, coding theorems, and block/conditional analyses for stochastic
communication sources.

## Common-schema mapping

Temporal restrictions appear in block probabilities and the entropy rate, not
necessarily in the one-symbol marginal. The quantity is a property of a
declared stochastic source and alphabet, not a physical carrier by itself.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

Coding assumptions, conditioning variables, stationarity, ergodicity, and
alphabet matter. Equal marginal entropy can conceal different future laws.
