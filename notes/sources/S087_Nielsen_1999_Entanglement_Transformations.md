# S087 — Nielsen (1999), Conditions for Entanglement Transformations

## Bibliographic record

- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.83.436>
- Primary manuscript: <https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9811053>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article

## Native result

A deterministic transformation between bipartite pure states by LOCC is
possible exactly when the Schmidt-coefficient vectors satisfy the stated
majorization relation.

## Common-schema mapping

Allowed operations induce a partial order on reachable states; a scalar
entanglement entropy does not determine that order.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The theorem is for bipartite pure states and deterministic LOCC. Other state
classes, probabilistic protocols, and operation families require other tests.
