## Why included

Records the framework proponents' own assessment of demonstrated, proposed,
and still-discriminating tests through June 2026. It is not counted as
independent support.

## Native contribution

The review surveys hybrid-system witnesses, photonic and NMR demonstrations,
thermodynamic proposals, Born-rule questions, biological applications, and
universal-constructor and cosmological proposals.

## Maturity and limitations

The review characterizes the 2022 homogenizer experiment as demonstrating
compatibility with unitary dynamics and calls for future experiments that
distinguish constructor-based irreversibility from standard entropy accounts.
It also records necessity gaps in the Born-rule program and the prospective
status of many broader tests.
