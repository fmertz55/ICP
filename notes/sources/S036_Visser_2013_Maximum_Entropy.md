## Why included

## Native result

Maximizing Shannon entropy subject to an expected logarithm constraint yields
`p_n proportional to n^(-z)`. Exact exponent one over infinite positive
integers is not normalizable, so finite support matters. Adding an expected
value constraint produces a power law with exponential cutoff.
