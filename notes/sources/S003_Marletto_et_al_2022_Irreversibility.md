# S003 — Marletto et al. (2022), Constructor-Based Irreversibility

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `MarlettoEtAl2022Irreversibility`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.128.080401>
- Status: peer-reviewed theory and experiment
- Stratum: constructor theory; quantum irreversibility

## Why included

Provides a rare model-and-experiment connection for constructor theory rather
than only a foundational proposal.

## Native result and method

Constructor-based irreversibility is expressed as a possible task whose inverse
is impossible in the constructor limit. The authors show compatibility with
time-reversal-symmetric quantum dynamics using a universal quantum homogenizer
and implement the model with high-quality single-photon qubits.

## Common-schema mapping

- `H`: repeated homogenizer interactions.
- `M`: joint states of the homogenizer units and target systems.
- `Pi`: specified preparations, interactions, and measurements.
- Future object: accuracy and repeatability of forward versus inverse tasks.
- Claim type: modal result with finite experimental observations.

## Assumptions, limitations, and rival reading

The construction is model-specific, and the ideal constructor property is
approached through finite resources. The experiment supports the physical
realizability of the model behavior; it is not a universal empirical test of
constructor theory or a measurement of ICP.

## ICP relationship

- Direction/directness: supports / partial.
- Useful bridge: distinguishes a repeatable transformation asymmetry from mere
  microscopic time-asymmetry.
- Break point: the result does not supply a general history-to-future
  probability law or prove irreducible memory.
