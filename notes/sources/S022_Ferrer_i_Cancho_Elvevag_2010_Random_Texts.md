# S022 — Ferrer-i-Cancho and Elvevåg (2010), Random Texts Do Not Exhibit the Real Zipf Distribution

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `FerrerCanchoElvevag2010RandomTexts`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0009411>
- Status: peer-reviewed simulation and empirical comparison
- Stratum: Zipf mechanisms; adversarial random-text test

## Why included

It prevents the useful random-typing null from being overstated as a full
explanation of empirical language.

## Native result

The authors simulate random texts at the same lengths as ten English texts,
including variants with target-text character probabilities. Random models
show plateaus, humps, and excessive vocabulary width and are inconsistent with
real texts under maximum, mean, and standard deviation of rank.

## Common-schema mapping

- Comparison: real sequences versus fitted memory-free character models.
- Future law: character IID in the null; no sequential semantic prediction.
- ICP status: rejects tested simple nulls but does not select ICP.

## Limitations and rival reading

Only declared random-text families and ten texts are tested. Failure of these
families does not establish a unique linguistic or physical mechanism.
