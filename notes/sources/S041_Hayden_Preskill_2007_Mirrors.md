# S041 — Hayden and Preskill (2007), Black Holes as Mirrors

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `HaydenPreskill2007Mirrors`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1088/1126-6708/2007/09/120>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article
- Stratum: quantum-information recovery and scrambling

## Native result

Under unitary, rapidly mixing black-hole dynamics and unlimited control of the
emitted radiation, newly deposited quantum information can be recovered
quickly after the entropy halfway point. Earlier deposited information remains
hidden until roughly that point.

## Common-schema mapping

The diary is the input, collected radiation and prior entanglement define the
accessible subsystem, and decoding fidelity is the future operational outcome.
The model cleanly separates global preservation from subsystem recovery.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

Rapid availability is conditional on typical scrambling, a known channel
model, and strong collection/decoding access. The theorem does not show that an
astrophysical observer can perform the decoder or identify a unique carrier.
