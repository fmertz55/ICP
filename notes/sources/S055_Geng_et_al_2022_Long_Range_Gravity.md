## Native result

The paper argues that a compact island in long-range gravity conflicts with
gravitational dressing and the Gauss law: energy of an island excitation can
be detected outside, whereas complementary entanglement-wedge algebras should
commute. Known examples evade the tension together with a massive graviton.

## Common-schema mapping

The proposed inside/outside algebras, not merely the geometry, determine
whether an island reconstruction claim is consistent.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

This is a strong qualification, not a universal no-go theorem accepted by all
sides. It applies to the paper's definition of islands and long-range-gravity
assumptions; later work contests whether relational operators and different
subalgebras evade it.
