# S072 — Crooks (1999), Entropy Production Fluctuation Theorem

## Bibliographic record

- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.60.2721>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article

## Native result

Under microscopically reversible stochastic dynamics, forward and conjugate
reverse work distributions obey a ratio determined by dissipated work.

## Common-schema mapping

Entropy production is a log ratio between two explicitly indexed path laws.
The reverse experiment, bath, protocol, and initial ensembles are constitutive.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

An incorrectly specified reverse process or hidden active dynamics invalidates
the operational interpretation. The ratio does not measure predictive memory.
