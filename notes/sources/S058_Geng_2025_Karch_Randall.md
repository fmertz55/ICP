## Native result

In a Karch–Randall braneworld with a nongravitating bath, the paper relates the
topological transition of a higher-dimensional RT surface to formation of a
replica-wormhole saddle on the brane. Explicit calculations are supplied in a
2+1-dimensional ambient setting, with a higher-dimensional extension.

## Common-schema mapping

The bath coupling, brane theory, and ambient holographic dual make the
observable subsystem and encoding map explicit. The Page curve is a property
of that constructed open system.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

This closes a derivational gap in a particular model; it is not evidence that
the same saddle or carrier operates in an isolated asymptotically flat black
hole.
