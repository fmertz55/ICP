## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `Mathur2009Paradox`

## Native result

Using strong subadditivity, Mathur argues that small corrections to the leading
Hawking pair state cannot reverse the growth of radiation–interior
entanglement. Under locality and a traditional smooth horizon, order-one
departures, remnants, or information loss remain necessary alternatives.

## Common-schema mapping

The result is a no-small-corrections constraint on candidate dynamics. The
article also advocates fuzzball microstates, but that programmatic conclusion
is not established by the inequality alone.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

This is useful as a falsification gate: a proposed carrier cannot restore
unitarity merely by invoking unspecified tiny correlations. It does not select
one microscopic resolution.
