# S044 — Harlow and Hayden (2013), Quantum Computation vs. Firewalls

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `HarlowHayden2013Computation`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP06(2013)085>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article
- Stratum: decoding complexity

## Native result

For broad classes of black holes and generic encodings, the decoding needed by
the AMPS thought experiment requires time exponential in black-hole entropy,
longer than the available evaporation time. The paper proves a hardness result
for generic initial states and motivates computationally protected locality.

## Common-schema mapping

Information may be globally present and in-principle reconstructable while
remaining inaccessible under a finite computational budget. The resource
bound is therefore part of the operational future law, not an afterthought.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The result blocks a class of observers; it does not prove an absolute decoding
lower bound for every structured black-hole dynamics or establish horizon
smoothness.
