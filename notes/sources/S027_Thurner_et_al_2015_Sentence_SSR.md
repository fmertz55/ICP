# S027 — Thurner et al. (2015), Sample-Space Collapse in Sentence Formation

## Bibliographic record

- Citation key: `ThurnerEtAl2015SentenceSSR`
- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1098/rsif.2015.0330>
- Status: peer-reviewed model and corpus analysis
- Stratum: Zipf mechanisms; language and SSR

## Why included

Provides sequence-level predictions—nested successor sets and directionality—
that can distinguish SSR from a marginal-only account.

## Native result

The paper models sentence production as successive restriction of possible
words. It estimates successor-set nestedness in ten books, links it to scaling,
and reports degradation of the relation under controls that destroy the
proposed directional organization.

## Common-schema mapping

- `H`: sentence prefix.
- `M`: current allowable successor support.
- Future law: conditional next-word support and weights.
- ICP status: path-sensitive candidate, not carrier evidence.

## Limitations and rival reading

Successor support is estimated from the same corpus, is binary, and may encode
grammar or topic. Held-out likelihood and a carrier intervention are absent.
