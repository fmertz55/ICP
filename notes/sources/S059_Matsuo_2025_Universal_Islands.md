## Native result

Within the paper's general semiclassical setup, the quantum extremal surface is
outside the horizon for Hartle–Hawking stationary states and inside the horizon
for evaporating Unruh states, largely independent of detailed black-hole
parameters.

## Common-schema mapping

The vacuum choice and evaporation history alter the QES location and thus the
reconstruction assignment. The result concerns extremal surfaces, not direct
information-flow trajectories.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

“Universal” is internal to the analyzed class and assumptions. The work does
not settle factorization, the microscopic encoding channel, decoding
complexity, or astrophysical observability.
