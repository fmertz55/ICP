## Native result

In wedge holography with a normalizable massless graviton, Kumar adds a unitary
corner-defect CFT whose entropy contributes to generalized entropy. A local
endpoint model then has a stable non-horizon saddle that can dominate late,
with positive couplings and no negative-DGP ghost.

## Common-schema mapping

The additional defect sector is a concrete new component of the encoding and
entropy functional. The example shows that graviton masslessness alone does
not logically forbid every Page-transition saddle.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The calculation is a local near-horizon endpoint model with an imposed
anchoring map. It does not supply a fully backreacted global RT solution,
operator-algebra/Gauss-law analysis, or realistic evaporation channel.
