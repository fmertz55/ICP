## Native result

Using infrared factorization, the authors argue that after appropriate dressing
of hard in/out states, the soft-dependent part of the S-matrix is essentially
trivial. The black-hole information problem can then be formulated entirely in
the dressed hard sector.

hard-state distinctions required for full reconstruction.
