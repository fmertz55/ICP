## Why included

Most direct current source for a matched-marginal sequence intervention: the
final counts are fixed while order is changed.

## Native result

Different token orderings with one frequency multiset can yield different
type-token curves. The paper constructs maximally accelerated and delayed
discovery envelopes and a one-parameter temporal model. Ordered-versus-shuffled
effects are small for their Gutenberg data and large for music and browsing.

## Common-schema mapping

- `H`: token order.
- `M`: final counts are held fixed; temporal organization varies.
- Future law: novelty arrival and discovery trajectory.
- ICP status: establishes path sensitivity, not embodiment.

## Limitations and rival reading

Reordering is an analytic surrogate, not a physical counterfactual. Domain
heterogeneity prevents a universal order-effect claim.
