# S074 — Still et al. (2012), Thermodynamics of Prediction

## Bibliographic record

- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.109.120604>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article

## Native result

For the paper's driven stochastic system, information about the past that is
not predictive of the future is tied to thermodynamic inefficiency.

## Common-schema mapping

The result distinguishes stored past information, predictive information, and
dissipation rather than equating them.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

The equality is model- and protocol-dependent and does not establish a
universal identity among information, memory, entropy production, and physical
constraint.
