## Native result

The authors argue that the complete asymptotic algebra already reconstructs the
black-hole interior, so a full-exterior fine-grained entropy does not exhibit
information “emergence.” A Page curve can be produced by restricting the
algebra—for example, a detector blind to part of the boundary—or by coupling a
nongravitating bath. They deny that the compact-island construction of S060
defines an exact fine-grained algebra in standard gravity.

## Common-schema mapping

The same global state supports different entropy curves after an explicit
change of observable algebra. This is a direct stress test of the `A` index in
ICP's operational core.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

This is the opposing side of a live dispute, not settled consensus. It also
distinguishes approximate relational locality from exact fine-grained
factorization and notes open questions for small AdS black holes.
