## Why this source is included

This is the primary formulation of Gravity from Entropy (GfE). It supplies the
native metric-operator logarithmic action motivating the proposed comparison
with IOTA's finite reachable-support measure. It is a field result and novelty
constraint, not evidence that ICP or IOTA has been established.

## Native system and question

The model is a continuum Lorentzian manifold with a topological metric acting
on the direct sum of 0-, 1-, and 2-form sectors. It compares the physical
spacetime metric with an induced metric containing matter and curvature
contributions. Variations of the metric, matter fields, and auxiliary fields
within the postulated action replace an experimental intervention family.

The source asks whether a local statistical-mechanics action built from the
relation between those metrics can yield a modified theory of gravity with an
Einstein limit.

## Native formalism and exact result

The flattened operator ratio and its inverse G-field are

`Theta = Gtilde gtilde^{-1}` and `Gcal = Theta^{-1}`,

and the logarithmic metric-operator term is

`L = -Tr_F ln Theta`.

Within the postulated model, varying the full action yields modified field
equations. Introducing the G-field gives a dressed Einstein-Hilbert form with
an emergent nonnegative G-field-dependent term. The reported field equations
contain at most second derivatives of the metric and G-field; in the stated
low-coupling regime the theory reduces to Einstein gravity with zero
cosmological constant.

The paper does not establish stability, canonical quantization, observational
viability, or uniqueness of the action.

## Common-schema mapping

- Physical carrier or organization: the local physical/induced metric-operator
  pair and G-field.
- Dynamics: Euler-Lagrange evolution from the postulated action.
- Native future object: solutions of the modified field equations, not an
  intervention-indexed future-law family.
- Reachable set or future-law distribution: not defined.
- Comparison: physical versus induced metric through a logarithmic operator
  ratio.

No history-formed record or carrier succession is defined. The GfE auxiliary
constraint is not the ICP carrier criterion.

## Assumptions, alternatives, and ICP relationship

The action is postulated. The metric operators are invertible but are not
trace-one probability states. Treating the auxiliary G-field as physical adds
model content beyond a formal multiplier rewrite. GfE may be a complete
relative-entropy-inspired modified-gravity model without an ICP history
variable. A shared logarithmic form need not imply a shared mechanism.

The native derivation is a `field_result`; the GfE--IOTA mapping is a
`synthesis_inference`. The relationship is partial and both supportive and
qualifying. ICP would still need history formation, carrier intervention,
succession, representation robustness, and a discriminating prediction.
