# S086 — König, Renner, and Schaffner (2009), Min- and Max-Entropy

## Bibliographic record

- DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2009.2025545>
- Primary manuscript: <https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.1338>
- Status: peer-reviewed theoretical article

## Native result

Conditional min- and max-entropies receive operational interpretations in
one-shot guessing, secrecy, entanglement fidelity, and decoupling tasks.

## Common-schema mapping

Side information, allowed operations, copy number, task, and smoothing error
select the relevant entropy.

## Boundary and ICP relationship

Asymptotic Shannon or von Neumann entropy need not predict finite-task
performance, and no computational-efficiency guarantee follows automatically.
