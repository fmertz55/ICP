## Why included

Canonical null explanation for why a reduced system can exhibit history
dependence even when a larger physical state follows ordinary dynamics.

## Native result and formalism

Projection onto selected macroscopic variables yields an exact generalized
kinetic equation with memory kernels and unresolved-variable contributions.

## Common-schema mapping

- `H`: prior trajectory of selected variables.
- `M`: unresolved degrees of freedom represented through the kernel.
- `Pi`: the analyst's relevant-variable projection.
- Future object: reduced probability evolution.

## Assumptions, limitations, and rival reading

Memory is relative to the projection. If omitted microscopic variables are
included, the enlarged description may be Markovian. That is a direct rival to
any claim that reduced non-Markovianity establishes a new fundamental
information principle.
