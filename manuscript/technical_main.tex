\author{Douglas Clark}

\begin{abstract}
The IOTA research program begins from an information-first substrate
hypothesis: present information is the causally retained result of interaction
history, embodied in current state and relational organization, and information
changes when interaction changes the structure of reachable futures. The
Information--Constraint Principle (ICP) proposes that physically effective
information is persistent, embodied constraint on an explicitly declared family
of possible or probable future responses. This article has two purposes: to
audit whether IOTA/ICP is scientifically viable in light of current theory,
experiment, close prior art, and project results; and, if so, to specify the
research path from constraint persistence to coherence, quasiparticles, fields,
and emergent mass and energy. We conduct a critical integrative synthesis rather
than a systematic review, separating established field results, project
constructive evidence, cross-domain inference, and ontology-level hypothesis.
Control and viability theory, computational mechanics, process tensors,
counterfactual semantic information, feedback thermodynamics, decoherence,
biological memory, adjacent-possible models, and emergent quasiparticle physics
establish strong native precedents for parts of the proposal. They also impose
a severe novelty test: generic claims that information changes future
possibilities or opens neighboring novelties are already covered by mature
formalisms. IOTA contributes bounded demonstrations of
constraint transport through disjoint carrier replacement, prospective lineage
succession, finite opportunity lifetimes, and realization redundancy. A new
exact specialization identifies its nested-uniform support reduction with
forward Kullback--Leibler divergence; its signed extension gives positive
forward divergence for nested pruning and negative reverse divergence for
nested expansion. The adjacent possible is therefore the novelty-bearing
sector of an indexed reachable-future family and close prior art, not
independent confirmation. The signed deformation supplies a candidate operator
bridge to Gravity from Entropy's log-determinant construction, while leaving
the operator map, continuum limit, and any distinctive cosmological prediction
unproved. A separate registered
construction recovers a bounded one-particle Dirac role,
but supplies the quantum substrate, clock, Born readout, and mass parameter.
Our verdict is positive but graded. IOTA/ICP has real scientific legs as a
coherent, falsifiable research program and operational synthesis, and the
project has demonstrated beginnings of its proposed persistence mechanism. It
has not yet established a distinct fundamental law, an IOTA-to-quantum
derivation, or an information-first ontology. The decisive next bridge is a
minimal persistent coherence that yields quantum composition and probability,
then a manipulable quasiparticle role, without importing the target structure.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\colorbox{icppale}{\parbox{0.965\linewidth}{\small
\textbf{Verdict in one sentence.} IOTA/ICP is viable enough to pursue and
specific enough to fail, but the present evidence supports a research program
and bounded mechanism---not yet a fundamental ontology or law.}}
