# Provisional manuscript outline

## Working title

**Information as Constraint on Physical Possibility: A Critical
Cross-Disciplinary Synthesis**

Alternative: **The Constrained Future: Scientific Foundations and Limits of a
Candidate Information-Constraint Principle**

## Scope note

This is a literature-only paper. The supplied project manuscript and IOTA
simulation results provide context but do not count as evidence. The contribution
must be the formal comparison, disanalogies, and novelty test—not a collection of
statements that information is physical.

## Sections

1. **Problem and scope** — State the candidate motif and the risk of conflating
   distinct meanings of information and constraint.
2. **Method** — Describe searches, eligibility, extraction, appraisal, and claim
   levels from the review protocol.
3. **Operational vocabulary** — Define the comparison axes and preserve
   field-specific meanings.
4. **Transformation and reachability** — Examine possible/impossible
   transformations, resources, and dynamical accessibility.
5. **Physical memory, selection, and records** — Examine decoherence,
   environmental records, non-Markovianity, and process descriptions.
6. **Predictive structure and emergent state** — Examine causal states,
   macrostates, coarse-graining, and observer/task dependence.
7. **Relational and recursively maintained organization** — Treat relational
   quantum approaches and biological organization with explicit interpretive and
   scale limitations.
8. **Cross-domain structural comparison** — Present the common-schema table and
   the strongest breaks in analogy.
9. **Rivals, redundancy, and counterevidence** — Test ordinary dynamics, state
   augmentation, observer dependence, and scale-specific explanations.
10. **What would make ICP a principle?** — Specify formal objects, novel
    implications, and discriminating tests.
11. **Graded conclusions** — Separate established results, synthesis inferences,
    and ICP conjectures.
