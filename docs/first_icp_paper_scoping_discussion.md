Yes—and it may be the **best first ICP paper**, because it can establish intellectual legitimacy without depending on the IOTA simulation results.

The defensible claim would not be:

> Existing science proves ICP.

It would be:

> Several mature but currently separate research programs converge on the proposition that the physically relevant content of a state lies in the constraints it inherits, preserves, and imposes on possible future transformations.

That is recognizably ICP, stated without overclaiming.

## Proposed paper

**Working title**

> **Information as Constraint on Physical Possibility: A Cross-Disciplinary Synthesis**

A sharper alternative:

> **The Constrained Future: Scientific Foundations for the Information Constraint Principle**

### Central thesis

> Across quantum foundations, open-system dynamics, statistical mechanics, computational mechanics, and biological organization, information increasingly appears not merely as a message or numerical quantity, but as physically instantiated structure that distinguishes possible from impossible—or probable from inaccessible—future transformations.

## The literature pillars

### 1. Constructor theory: physics expressed through possible and impossible transformations

This is the closest existing conceptual relative.

Constructor theory proposes expressing physical law in terms of which transformations are possible, impossible, or achievable only under particular conditions. Its constructor theory of information treats information as a physical property characterized by permitted transformations rather than as an abstract substance. Recent work is moving toward explicit experimental tests. 

**ICP connection:** inherited physical organization determines the reachable transformation set.

**Important distinction:** ICP would add temporal inheritance, realized histories, repair possibilities, and the progressive structuring of reachable futures.

---

### 2. Decoherence and quantum Darwinism: interactions select persistent states and records

Decoherence does not simply make quantum information disappear. System–environment interaction selectively preserves certain stable pointer states while suppressing incompatible coherences. Quantum Darwinism extends this by showing how environmental fragments acquire redundant records of selected system states, making those states effectively objective and persistent. 

**ICP connection:** not every mathematically available state remains equally reachable or retainable. Interaction history selects and reinforces a constrained family of stable continuations.

This is extremely close to your idea that persistence begins when interactions create structures capable of carrying forward constraints.

---

### 3. Non-Markovian quantum dynamics: the past remains physically active

Process-tensor approaches represent an open quantum system’s future behavior as dependent not merely on its present reduced state, but on previous interactions and interventions. Current work explicitly treats quantum memory as something that can be identified and used to predict later dynamics. 

**ICP connection:** the realized past alters the current space of possible responses. History is not merely a record; it is encoded in the effective causal structure governing continuation.

This may be the strongest formal literature bridge to your phrase:

> The realized future is conditioned by organized structure inherited from prior interactions.

---

### 4. Computational mechanics: the present is the information from the past that constrains the future

Computational mechanics groups past histories into **causal states** when those histories produce the same probability distribution over futures. Its causal-state representation is the minimal structure required for optimal prediction. Related work describes the information stored in the present as the shared information connecting past and future. 

This is almost a mathematical counterpart to ICP:

> Two histories are informationally equivalent when they constrain the same reachable future distribution.

It also gives us considerably better terminology than calling all future possibilities “eigenstates.” They are more properly a **conditional future distribution**, **future morph**, or **reachable successor set**.

---

### 5. Macrostates and emergence: dynamically meaningful states predict their own continuation

Work connecting computational mechanics to statistical mechanics argues that objective macrostates can be understood as states that preserve enough information to predict future macroscopic behavior. They are not arbitrary summaries; they arise from the system’s dynamics and observational relationships. 

**ICP connection:** information becomes physically significant when it changes which later states remain dynamically reachable.

This supports the crucial distinction between:

- raw microscopic detail;
- Shannon information;
- and **causally effective constraint information**.

ICP is principally about the third.

---

### 6. Relational quantum approaches: correlations may be more fundamental than isolated properties

Relational formulations treat quantum states and properties as meaningful relative to other physical systems rather than as wholly intrinsic attributes. Several formulations emphasize correlation information as more fundamental than isolated subsystem properties. Quantum-reference-frame work further shows that superposition and entanglement descriptions may depend on physical reference frames. 

**ICP connection:** an iota’s informational meaning would not reside solely inside it. Meaning arises from how its state constrains and is constrained by surrounding relations.

That fits the emerging lesson from IOTA: the payload may be less important than relational placement and interaction structure.

---

### 7. Biological organization: constraints become recursively maintained

This section should be narrower and carefully sourced. The relevant biological literature concerns organization, constraint closure, autocatalysis, heredity, and the capacity of existing structure to channel later state transitions.

The argument would not be that biology proves fundamental ICP. It would show that once self-maintaining systems appear, physical constraints can become recursively preserved, reproduced, and used to generate further constraints. Recent reviews continue to describe life through convergent physical and informational organizational principles. 

**ICP connection:** biology is where constraint persistence becomes constraint recursion—information about how to preserve and reproduce information-bearing organization.

## The paper’s most important sentence

I would build the entire review toward something like this:

> Contemporary science does not yet establish that information is ontologically fundamental, but it repeatedly identifies physically effective information with the structure that links histories to differentiated sets of possible futures.

That is a strong, honest claim.

## What we must avoid

The paper should not:

- claim that quantum mechanics says “everything is information”;
- identify every physical state as an eigenstate;
- treat Shannon information as a physical causal agent;
- imply that constructor theory, decoherence, or computational mechanics already formulates ICP;
- use the IOTA findings as evidence, since this would be a literature-only paper;
- claim emergence of mass, energy, or the wave function.

Instead, it should distinguish three levels:

1. **Established science:** prior states, correlations, environments, and constraints affect future distributions.
2. **Cross-disciplinary synthesis:** these results share a common constraint-based structure.
3. **ICP hypothesis:** this common structure reflects a general principle concerning inherited information and physically realizable futures.

## A good abstract

:::writing{variant="document" id="41827"}
Information is conventionally treated as a quantity associated with uncertainty, communication, or description. Across several areas of contemporary science, however, information also plays a more explicitly physical role: it distinguishes possible from impossible transformations, records prior interactions, identifies states with equivalent future distributions, and supports structures that persist through environmental exchange. This article reviews convergent developments in constructor theory, quantum decoherence and Darwinism, non-Markovian process theory, computational mechanics, statistical emergence, relational quantum theory, and biological organization. We show that these fields share a recurring structural motif: the physically relevant information present in a system is the organization inherited from its history that constrains its accessible future states and transformations. We call this synthesis the Information Constraint Principle. The review does not argue that existing results prove information to be ontologically fundamental. Rather, it identifies a common scientific foundation for treating information as physically instantiated constraint and specifies the additional formal and empirical requirements needed to elevate the synthesis into a testable physical principle.
:::

This could credibly be a substantial review or perspective paper. The real contribution would be the **synthesis and formal comparison table**, not merely collecting quotations saying “information is physical.”
