# Pilot Batch 08: emergent quasiparticles and the IOTA-to-physics ladder

Search cutoff: 2026-07-20

## Question

Does current science establish that local relational or lattice degrees of
freedom can organize into excitations with particle-like roles, and what does
that precedent do---and not do---for the IOTA claim that familiar physics is
emergent?

## Bounded answer

Yes, established theory and experiment show that collective organizations of
more microscopic degrees of freedom can behave as quasiparticles with charge,
statistics, dispersion, braiding, and other particle-like properties. Exactly
solvable string-net and topological-order models provide explicit existence
constructions; condensed-matter and quantum-simulator experiments exhibit or
engineer fractionalized excitations; and one-dimensional quantum cellular
automata can approach free-fermion quantum field theory in a declared
long-wavelength limit.

This supports the **logical possibility** of the IOTA emergence ladder. It does
not show that the current IOTA lattice produces quantum amplitudes, that its
coherences are physical quasiparticles, or that known elementary particles are
quasiparticles of an informational substrate.

## Minimum balanced sources

### Levin and Wen (2005), string-net condensation

- Primary model: *String-net condensation: A physical mechanism for
  topological phases*, Physical Review B 71, 045110.
  DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.71.045110>.
- Result: exactly soluble local spin Hamiltonians possess string-net-condensed
  ground states; in three dimensions the framework can support emergent gauge
  bosons and fermions.
- ICP relevance: an explicit existence proof that local quantum relational
  organization can carry collective particle-like roles.
- Boundary: the construction assumes quantum Hilbert spaces, Hamiltonians,
  tensor-category structure, and a chosen phase. It does not derive quantum
  theory or select our vacuum from non-quantum informational primitives.

### Kitaev (2006), honeycomb fractionalization

- Primary model: *Anyons in an exactly solved model and beyond*, Annals of
  Physics 321, 2--111.
  DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aop.2005.10.005>.
- Result: an exactly solvable local spin model supports fractionalized Majorana
  fermion and gauge-flux excitations in appropriate phases.
- ICP relevance: microscopic carrier variables and effective excitation roles
  need not coincide.
- Boundary: the quasiparticle content follows from a supplied quantum spin
  Hamiltonian and phase structure.

### Jansa et al. (2018), material evidence for fractional excitations

- Primary experiment: *Observation of two types of fractional excitation in
  the Kitaev honeycomb magnet*, Nature Physics 14, 786--790.
  DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41567-018-0129-5>.
- Result: NMR gap behavior in alpha-RuCl3 was interpreted as contributions from
  Majorana fermion and gauge-flux excitations across a range of fields and
  temperatures.
- ICP relevance: independent physical precedent for collective excitation
  roles not identical to microscopic constituents.
- Boundary: identification is model-dependent and does not establish an
  information-first substrate.

### Iqbal et al. (2024), controlled anyonic roles on a processor

- Primary experiment: *Non-Abelian topological order and anyons on a trapped-ion
  processor*, Nature 626, 505--511.
  DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06934-4>.
- Result: an adaptive circuit prepared a 27-qubit D4 topological-order state
  with reported per-site fidelity above 98.4 percent and used interferometry to
  probe non-Abelian braiding.
- ICP relevance: a bounded laboratory construction in which a distributed
  quantum organization realizes manipulable particle-like topological roles.
- Boundary: the quantum computer, gates, measurement rules, and target order
  are engineered inputs; this is simulation/realization, not ontological
  emergence.

### Dhar et al. (2025), anyonization of bosons

- Primary experiment: *Observing anyonization of bosons in a quantum gas*,
  Nature 642, 53--57.
  DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09016-9>.
- Result: a one-dimensional strongly interacting cesium gas with a mobile spin
  impurity exhibited tunable anyonic correlations and dynamical
  fermionization.
- ICP relevance: the same microscopic bosonic constituents can realize
  different effective statistical behavior through organized correlations.
- Boundary: the anyonic correlation structure is engineered within ordinary
  quantum many-body physics; it does not explain why quantum theory holds.

### Mlodinow and Brun (2020), QCA-to-QFT construction and no-go boundary

- Primary theory: *Quantum field theory from a quantum cellular automaton in one
  spatial dimension and a no-go theorem in higher dimensions*, Physical Review
  A 102, 042211.
  DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.102.042211>.
- Result: a one-dimensional multiparticle QCA matches the associated quantum
  walk in the one-particle sector and approaches free-fermion QFT at long
  wavelength. The same class does not extend straightforwardly to two or more
  spatial dimensions.
- ICP relevance: the strongest direct precedent for the proposed rung from a
  local quantum update to field behavior.
- Boundary: it assumes quantum degrees of freedom and exposes a dimensional
  obstruction rather than a generic derivation.

### IOTA quantum-Dirac project result (2026)

- Project construction: two separately registered local quantum update
  specifications recover an intact shared core of a bounded 1+1-dimensional
  one-particle Dirac operational role.
- Positive contribution: frozen intervention maps, convergence and witness
  gates, explicit resources, single-use confirmation, independent numerical
  reconstruction, and disclosure of a shared empirical zone-edge predicate
  defect.
- Boundary: the two free blocks are time-frame related after mass-angle
  remapping; the quantum substrate, Born readout, clock, and mass schedule are
  supplied. The result is role recovery, not quasiparticle emergence from the
  base IOTA lattice.

## Cross-source conclusion

The literature defeats the blanket objection that particle-like physics cannot
emerge from local organized substrates. It does not close IOTA's actual burden.
That burden is to construct the quantum organization from its own declared base
primitives without hiding Hilbert space, complex amplitudes, composition,
probability, statistics, or the target Hamiltonian in the implementation.

The decisive next object is not a visually persistent blob. It is an excitation
with prospectively declared:

1. creation and annihilation operations;
2. localization and finite propagation;
3. dispersion and a refinement/continuum domain;
4. composition and exchange behavior;
5. symmetry and conserved quantities;
6. scattering or response to controlled perturbations;
7. persistence under carrier replacement and representation changes; and
8. a demonstration that these roles were not supplied as primitive target
   structure.

## Strongest nulls

- **Effective-theory null:** quasiparticle behavior is fully explained by the
  supplied microscopic Hamiltonian; ICP adds no variable or theorem.
- **Imported-quantum null:** the construction assumes the quantum structure it
  claims to derive.
- **Lattice-artifact null:** the role fails outside a narrow low-momentum,
  low-dimensional, or finite-time window.
- **Encoding null:** a different encoding or coarse-graining removes the claimed
  particle identity.
- **Universality null:** one engineered rule realizes one target without a
  class-level theorem, predictive selection principle, or independent physical
  consequence.

## Batch verdict

**Supported:** local quantum organization can produce genuine emergent
quasiparticle roles, and local QCA can recover bounded continuum particle/field
behavior.

**Not supported:** current IOTA coherences are physical quasiparticles; quantum
theory, mass, or energy has been derived from IOTA; or the universe is a
particular lattice.

**Effect on ICP:** raises the plausibility of the emergence ladder and sharpens
its gates, but does not raise the ontology verdict beyond open.
