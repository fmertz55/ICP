# Working synthesis

This is an evolving critical synthesis, not a repository of favorable
quotations. The detailed first result is in
`pilot_batch_01_formal_core.md`; the first mechanism-discrimination result is in
`pilot_batch_02_zipf_mechanism_discrimination.md`. Every statement below is
traceable through `../analysis/claim_evidence_matrix.csv`.

## 1. Operational vocabulary

- **Information** is not one cross-domain object. In the pilot it can mean a
  copyable physical variable, predictive mutual information, a causal-state
  statistic, an environmental record, or multi-time process correlation.
- **Hard possibility** concerns whether a declared repeatable transformation is
  physically achievable under stated resources.
- **Path support** concerns neighborhoods of trajectories with nonzero measure
  under a specified finite-horizon law. It is not identical to task possibility.
- **Soft constraint** is a reweighting within a reachable support.
- **Predictive state** is an equivalence class of histories that preserves a
  selected future distribution.
- **Operational memory** is dependence of future statistics on earlier controls
  after a declared present-state intervention, relative to a boundary and
  instrument set.
- **Counterfactual** is reserved for alternatives linked by a structural causal
  or potential-outcome model; multiple forecasts are not automatically
  counterfactuals.

## 2. What the pilot fields establish

Constructor theory establishes a physical, resource-relative can/cannot
language for repeatable tasks and information media. It does not supply a
history-conditioned stochastic process. Constructor-theoretic probability adds
decision-supporting weights under restrictive conditions, not an objective path
law.

Quantum process theory establishes complete multi-time intervention-response
statistics within a declared operational span. Causal breaks test whether the
earlier history remains relevant after resetting the reduced system. Quantum
Markov order and memory-carrier taxonomies show that results depend on the
instrument family and system partition. Superconducting-qubit tomography gives
direct empirical evidence for non-Markov multi-time correlations, but not a
unique microscopic carrier.

Computational mechanics establishes minimal optimal predictive states. Its
epsilon-transducer extension handles whole families of output laws under future
inputs. These results are close prior art for ICP but do not make the inferred
states physically embodied or Pearl-causal.

Macrostate and path-ensemble work supplies disciplined coarse-graining and
trajectory-distribution tools. It also supplies strong nulls: imposed
statistical constraints need not be material mechanisms, and projected-out
variables can generate all apparent memory.

The Zipf literature establishes a decisive anti-inference result: the same
rank-frequency marginal can arise from memory-free sampling, preferential
growth, latent mixtures, optimization, critical-like models, maximum-entropy
constraints, or history-dependent support reduction. Zipf therefore becomes
ICP-relevant only through conditional future-law and intervention predictions
beyond the marginal. Rank-count, count-distribution, and type-token laws must
remain distinct.

## 3. Common-schema comparison

The narrowest shared object surviving the pilot is:

`E_tau(m; Pi, A, B) = {P^pi_A(F[0:tau] | m, B) : pi in Pi}`.

`Pi` is the intervention family, `A` the observable algebra, and `B` the system
boundary, scale, resolution, and resource declaration. Omitting those indices
turns relational results into unjustified intrinsic claims.

The mapping is formal for process tensors and predictive-state models,
structural for constructor theory, contextual for causal semantics, and only
analogical when a statistical inference constraint is treated as a physical
carrier.

## 4. Strongest candidate recurring motif

Realized histories can leave present distinctions that are sufficient for
different operational future laws. The motif becomes an ICP candidate only when
the distinction is physically located, formed through history, manipulable, and
not eliminated by an adequate state description.

This is a supported synthesis inference, not evidence for an ontologically
fundamental principle.

## 5. Non-equivalences and boundary conditions

- Possible task ≠ finite-time path support.
- Zero probability of one continuous trajectory ≠ impossibility.
- Predictive equivalence ≠ intervention equivalence.
- Input conditioning ≠ surgical intervention without an assignment model.
- Interventional marginals ≠ a joint unit-level counterfactual distribution.
- Non-Markovian reduced dynamics ≠ irreducible history.
- Redundant environmental record ≠ partition-invariant objectivity.
- Constraint in Maximum Caliber ≠ embodied physical constraint.

## 6. Competing explanations

The complete-state ordinary-dynamics null (`R001`) and the state-augmentation
null (`R002`) are currently strongest. Observer/task dependence (`R003`) and the
epistemic-summary account (`R005`) remain live. Pilot evidence strengthens these
rivals rather than dismissing them, which sets the burden of proof for ICP.

## 7. What ICP may add

The current candidate contribution is an **embodied operational equivalence
criterion**: a present physical record `M` is sufficient for the complete
admissible intervention-response family, is produced through controlled history,
changes the family when its carrier is changed, and is stable across equivalent
descriptions or explicitly scoped to a declared representation.

This remains an ICP hypothesis. It will be novel only if it yields an invariant,
compression, prediction, prohibition, or carrier intervention beyond the best
complete-state process model.

## 8. Formal and empirical requirements

Use criteria `F001`–`F013`. The general priority design is:

1. prepare the same declared macrostate through distinct histories;
2. randomize matched interventions;
3. estimate out-of-sample future path laws;
4. enlarge the state until ordinary missing-variable memory is exhausted;
5. perturb the proposed carrier with material and energetic controls;
6. test representation and partition robustness.

## 9. Graded conclusions

- **Established field results:** task possibility, process tensors, causal
  breaks, predictive causal states, controlled epsilon-transducers,
  projection-induced memory, and the prediction/intervention distinction.
- **Supported synthesis:** the common formal recurrence is an indexed family of
  future laws, not a scalar information quantity.
- **Qualified:** physical embodiment, partition stability, and causal carrier
  status are not supplied by predictive or process sufficiency alone.
- **ICP conjecture:** an embodied record can be defined and tested as the minimal
  stable state sufficient for an intervention-response family.
- **Rejected as presently redundant:** merely renaming causal states, process
  tensors, or task possibility as ICP.
- **Zipf result:** a slope is rejected as ICP evidence; matched-marginal future-
  law discrimination is retained as a falsifiable laboratory, with a PSA
  sequence experiment preregistered but not yet executed.
- **Unresolved:** a new invariant or theorem, biological maintenance and repair,
  relational observables, a physical carrier test for any sequence result, and
  the black-hole accessibility/recoverability frontier.
