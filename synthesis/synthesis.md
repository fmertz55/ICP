## 1. Operational vocabulary

- **Information** is not one cross-domain object. In the pilot it can mean a
  copyable physical variable, predictive mutual information, a causal-state
  statistic, an environmental record, or multi-time process correlation.
- **Hard possibility** concerns whether a declared repeatable transformation is
  physically achievable under stated resources.
- **Path support** concerns neighborhoods of trajectories with nonzero measure
  under a specified finite-horizon law. It is not identical to task possibility.
- **Soft constraint** is a reweighting within a reachable support.
- **Predictive state** is an equivalence class of histories that preserves a
  selected future distribution.
- **Operational memory** is dependence of future statistics on earlier controls
  after a declared present-state intervention, relative to a boundary and
  instrument set.
- **Counterfactual** is reserved for alternatives linked by a structural causal
  or potential-outcome model; multiple forecasts are not automatically
  counterfactuals.
- **Operational information hierarchy** separates global preservation,
  algebra-relative distinguishability, logical reconstruction, allowed message
  recovery, and efficient recovery under declared resources.
- **Entropy profile** separates support volume, path-growth rate, typical
  randomness production, predictive memory, rare-event cost, entropy
  production, and allowed-transformation monotones. None is an unindexed
  synonym for constraint.

## 2. What the pilot fields establish

Constructor theory establishes a physical, resource-relative can/cannot
language for repeatable tasks and information media. It does not supply a
history-conditioned stochastic process. Constructor-theoretic probability adds
decision-supporting weights under restrictive conditions, not an objective path
law.

Quantum process theory establishes complete multi-time intervention-response
statistics within a declared operational span. Causal breaks test whether the
earlier history remains relevant after resetting the reduced system. Quantum
Markov order and memory-carrier taxonomies show that results depend on the
instrument family and system partition. Superconducting-qubit tomography gives
direct empirical evidence for non-Markov multi-time correlations, but not a
unique microscopic carrier.

Computational mechanics establishes minimal optimal predictive states. Its
epsilon-transducer extension handles whole families of output laws under future
inputs. These results are close prior art for ICP but do not make the inferred
states physically embodied or Pearl-causal.

Macrostate and path-ensemble work supplies disciplined coarse-graining and
trajectory-distribution tools. It also supplies strong nulls: imposed
statistical constraints need not be material mechanisms, and projected-out
variables can generate all apparent memory.

The Zipf literature establishes a decisive anti-inference result: the same
rank-frequency marginal can arise from memory-free sampling, preferential
growth, latent mixtures, optimization, critical-like models, maximum-entropy
constraints, or history-dependent support reduction. Zipf therefore becomes
ICP-relevant only through conditional future-law and intervention predictions
beyond the marginal. Rank-count, count-distribution, and type-token laws must
remain distinct.

The black-hole literature establishes a second anti-inference result: the same
Page-shaped entropy curve does not identify the same carrier, channel,
observable algebra, reconstruction map, or recovery cost. Controlled QES,
island, replica-wormhole, and holographic-QEC results are strong within their
declared models. They do not constitute a microscopic evaporation channel or an
observation of information recovery from an astrophysical black hole. The
2025–2026 massless-gravity dispute is best represented as an algebra- and
definition-sensitive disagreement, not as a settled verdict for or against
islands.

The entropy literature establishes a third anti-inference result: the same
scalar entropy can accompany different support languages, predictive memories,
state-conversion preorders, observable algebras, and control-limited futures.
Conversely, adding a physical constraint does not have a universal sign across
all entropy notions. A monotonic decrease follows only for a specified nested
set, fixed measure or reference, fixed algebra, and fixed functional. In
thermodynamic accessibility, resource theory, and current informational-
equilibrium work, the allowed-transition or channel structure is logically
prior to its entropic monotones.

## 3. Common-schema comparison

The narrowest shared object surviving the pilot is:

`E_tau(m; Pi, A, B) = {P^pi_A(F[0:tau] | m, B) : pi in Pi}`.

`Pi` is the intervention family, `A` the observable algebra, and `B` the system
boundary, scale, resolution, and resource declaration. Omitting those indices
turns relational results into unjustified intrinsic claims.

The mapping is formal for process tensors and predictive-state models,
structural for constructor theory, contextual for causal semantics, and only
analogical when a statistical inference constraint is treated as a physical
carrier.

## 4. Strongest candidate recurring motif

Realized histories can leave present distinctions that are sufficient for
different operational future laws. The motif becomes an ICP candidate only when
the distinction is physically located, formed through history, manipulable, and
not eliminated by an adequate state description.

This is a supported synthesis inference, not evidence for an ontologically
fundamental principle.

## 5. Non-equivalences and boundary conditions

- Possible task ≠ finite-time path support.
- Zero probability of one continuous trajectory ≠ impossibility.
- Predictive equivalence ≠ intervention equivalence.
- Input conditioning ≠ surgical intervention without an assignment model.
- Interventional marginals ≠ a joint unit-level counterfactual distribution.
- Non-Markovian reduced dynamics ≠ irreducible history.
- Redundant environmental record ≠ partition-invariant objectivity.
- Constraint in Maximum Caliber ≠ embodied physical constraint.
- Global information preservation ≠ distinguishability by a declared algebra.
- Algebra-relative distinguishability ≠ logical-algebra reconstruction.
- Logical reconstruction ≠ message recovery by an allowed decoder.
- Allowed recovery ≠ efficient recovery within time, energy, precision, and
  copy budgets.
- Page curve ≠ microscopic channel or carrier.
- Euclidean replica saddle ≠ Lorentzian information trajectory.
- Physical constraint ≠ variational inference constraint.
- One-symbol entropy ≠ entropy rate ≠ predictive memory.
- Topological path growth ≠ measure/KS entropy.
- Entropy production ≠ stored organization.
- Rare-event conditioning ≠ causal intervention.
- Equal entropy ≠ equal convertibility under allowed operations.
- Regional von Neumann entropy ≠ an algebra-independent property.
- A fitted Rényi or Tsallis index ≠ a mechanism.
- Algorithmic complexity ≠ a computable thermodynamic entropy.

## 6. Competing explanations

The complete-state ordinary-dynamics null (`R001`) and the state-augmentation
null (`R002`) are currently strongest across the program. Observer/task
dependence (`R003`) and the epistemic-summary account (`R005`) remain live. In
black-hole physics, the complete-asymptotic-algebra rival (`R016`), controlled-
model scope rival (`R017`), and resource-bounded recovery rival (`R018`) are
mandatory. Pilot evidence strengthens these rivals rather than dismissing them,
which sets the burden of proof for ICP.

## 7. What ICP may add

The current candidate contribution is an **embodied operational equivalence
criterion**: a present physical record `M` is sufficient for the complete
admissible intervention-response family, is produced through controlled history,
changes the family when its carrier is changed, and is stable across equivalent
descriptions or explicitly scoped to a declared representation.

This remains an ICP hypothesis. It will be novel only if it yields an invariant,
compression, prediction, prohibition, or carrier intervention beyond the best
complete-state process model.

## 8. Formal and empirical requirements

Use criteria `F001`–`F020`. The general priority design is:

1. prepare the same declared macrostate through distinct histories;
2. randomize matched interventions;
3. estimate out-of-sample future path laws;
4. enlarge the state until ordinary missing-variable memory is exhausted;
5. perturb the proposed carrier with material and energetic controls;
6. test representation and partition robustness.
7. where entropy is used, match the ordinary scalar first and test the full
   future-law, algebra, preorder, or entropy-profile difference that remains.

## 9. Graded conclusions

- **Established field results:** task possibility, process tensors, causal
  breaks, predictive causal states, controlled epsilon-transducers,
  projection-induced memory, and the prediction/intervention distinction.
- **Supported synthesis:** the common formal recurrence is an indexed family of
  future laws, not a scalar information quantity.
- **Qualified:** physical embodiment, partition stability, and causal carrier
  status are not supplied by predictive or process sufficiency alone.
- **ICP conjecture:** an embodied record can be defined and tested as the minimal
  stable state sufficient for an intervention-response family.
- **Rejected as presently redundant:** merely renaming causal states, process
  tensors, or task possibility as ICP.
- **Zipf result:** a slope is rejected as ICP evidence; matched-marginal future-
  law discrimination is retained as a falsifiable laboratory, with a PSA
  sequence experiment preregistered but not yet executed.
- **Black-hole result:** a Page curve is rejected as carrier evidence; the
  five-level preservation-to-efficient-recovery hierarchy and explicit
  algebra/resource indices are retained as durable constraints on ICP.
- **Entropy result:** “constraint lowers entropy” is rejected as a general ICP
  law; constraint is retained as the upstream restriction on states, paths,
  observables, or operations, with entropies serving as task-indexed
  projections or monotones.
- **Unresolved:** a new invariant or theorem, biological maintenance and repair,
  relational observables, a physical carrier test for any sequence result, a
  nonperturbative single-theory evaporation channel for realistic gravity, and
  the operational status of complete versus proper radiation algebras.

## 10. Bounded closure: control, causal information, and thermodynamics

Reachability, viability, empowerment, causal entropy, information flow,
counterfactual semantic information, and feedback thermodynamics jointly
preempt the generic claim that information or present organization changes
future options. The Muruga et al. 2026 preprint is a close physical analogy:
memory hidden from a visible coordinate can later support work extraction, but
an enlarged state restores an ordinary Markovian/free-energy account.

The remaining defensible novelty target is narrower: an interaction forms a
physical constraint role; its original witness ceases to instantiate the role;
a successor preserves a registered intervention-response family; and editing
that successor changes the future under matched controls. This target is not
yet a general field result.

## 11. Bounded closure: quantum records, frames, and causal order

Decoherence and Quantum Darwinism support interaction-generated records in
secondary physical carriers, including controlled demonstrations of redundant
imprints. The evidence ladder must remain explicit:

`decoherence != pointer selection != redundant encoding != accessible consensus
!= spectrum-broadcast objectivity != inherited future constraint`.

Factorization is a major null because the redundantly recorded observable can
depend on the system/environment decomposition. Relational quantum mechanics
and quantum reference frames impose relational and operational discipline but
are non-equivalent programs and do not add an accumulated memory law. Likewise,
controlled gate order, causal nonseparability, causal-inequality violation,
indefinite spacetime structure, and emergent causality are different claims.

The decisive bridge is a carrier preserve/erase/swap/scramble intervention with
matched present state and a process-family or reachable-set readout. Complete
standard quantum dynamics on the enlarged state remains the leading null.

## 12. Bounded closure: biological organization

CRISPR immunity, chromatin memory, epistatic path dependence, autocatalytic
networks, and synthetic compartments provide strong examples of
history-produced, physically embodied organization that changes later
responses. They also demonstrate component turnover and carrier editing without
requiring carrier-free information. Ordinary genetic, chemical, energetic, and
kinetic explanations are successful native accounts.

Biology therefore supports ICP as a scale-bounded experimental program. It
does not establish a universal physical principle or information-first
ontology. Counterfactual semantic information and causal-leverage density are
especially close prior art because they already intervene on information-
bearing structure and measure changes in future viability or trajectory
distributions.

## 13. Bounded closure: quasiparticle emergence

String-net condensation, topological order, quantum simulators, and lattice
field constructions establish that local quantum organization can generate
collective excitations with particle-like statistics, charges, transport, and
effective field roles. This is a genuine existence proof for a downstream
emergence rung. It is not a derivation from a pre-quantum IOTA substrate because
the cited constructions begin with quantum state structure and dynamics.

The appropriate IOTA test is consequently operational: creation/annihilation,
dispersion, composition, symmetry, conservation, scattering, robustness, and
scaling must be recovered from a previously derived quantum coherence rather
than inserted as a target map.

## 14. Final viability verdict and path

**IOTA/ICP has real scientific legs as a coherent, falsifiable research program
and operational synthesis. It is not yet an established fundamental law or
ontology.** Bounded IOTA interventions demonstrate beginnings of constraint
transport and succession; the Dirac project demonstrates a later role under
supplied quantum structure; PSA supplies project evidence for relational
measurement discipline. None is independent validation of the substrate.

The forward ladder is:

1. replicate carrier succession across laws, schedulers, topologies, and
   representations;
2. isolate a minimal persistent coherence;
3. derive quantum composition, dynamics, and probability without importing
   them;
4. construct a manipulable quasiparticle role;
5. recover interacting field roles and close lattice no-go boundaries;
6. derive mass- and energy-like invariants; and
7. recover operational geometry, clocks, and a distinguishing physical
   prediction.

The program should narrow or stop if complete-state augmentation repeatedly
removes the historical variable, matched carrier edits have no effect,
reasonable representation changes destroy the role, or the quantum and field
targets must be supplied rather than derived.
