# Bounded literature closure summary

Evidence cutoff: 2026-07-20

Review type: adversarial critical-scoping synthesis, not a systematic review or
meta-analysis.

## Closure decision

The literature base is sufficiently closed to write the present viability and
framing papers. Additional searching is unlikely to change the manuscript's
central verdict without a more specific theorem, experiment, or bridge claim.
The closure is conceptual, not exhaustive: the retained sources cover the
strongest positive mechanisms, closest formal rivals, decisive
non-equivalences, and current frontier objections needed for the present
question.

## What the closure establishes for drafting

- Generic claims that present information changes future possibilities are
  already covered by mature native formalisms.
- Information can persist beyond an original bearer in other physical
  carriers; no reviewed result requires carrier-free information.
- History-built physical organization can change later intervention responses
  in quantum, thermodynamic, and biological systems.
- Collective quantum systems can realize particle-like roles, but those
  existence proofs begin with quantum structure.
- Zipf laws, entropy values, and Page curves do not identify a unique carrier
  or mechanism.
- IOTA/ICP remains scientifically viable only at the narrower target of
  interaction-formed, physically embodied constraint-role succession that
  survives carrier intervention, complete-state nulls, and reasonable changes
  of representation.
