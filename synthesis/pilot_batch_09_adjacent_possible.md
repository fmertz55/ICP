# Pilot Batch 09: the adjacent possible and signed possibility-space flux

Search cutoff: 2026-07-21

## Question

Does the adjacent-possible literature already formalize the claim that realized
history changes which novelties can occur next, and, if so, what does that
precedent support---and make non-novel---in ICP and IOTA?

## Bounded answer

The correlation is unusually strong. Kauffman's adjacent possible is native
prior art for the novelty-bearing part of an ICP reachable-future set: outcomes
that are not yet actual but are accessible in one declared step from what is
actual. Later urn, network, social, combinatorial, higher-order, and scaffolded
models make history-dependent opening of opportunities mathematically explicit.
Some reproduce correlated novelty, Heaps- and Zipf-like scaling, network
structure, or plateau-to-explosive growth within their assumptions.

That overlap is a **formal bridge and novelty constraint**, not independent
confirmation of ICP. The literature already contains triggered support growth,
constraint-enabled novelty, evolving discovery networks, and combinatorial
expansion. Heaps' law, Zipf's law, novelty clustering, and TAP ``hockey-stick''
growth are therefore not unique signatures of ICP. Conversely, most formal
adjacent-possible models predeclare an alphabet, urn rule, graph, string grammar,
or combinatorial generator. They model evolving accessibility or first discovery
inside that construction; they do not establish the stronger claim that nature
creates ontically new, intrinsically unprestatable phase-space dimensions.

The remaining defensible ICP opportunity is narrower and causal: a history forms
an embodied carrier; controlled alteration or replacement of that carrier changes
a prospectively declared adjacent frontier; the enabled, lost, and retained
possibilities are robust across reasonable classifiers and scales; and the role
survives succession across physical realizations. Existing IOTA Findings 068--070
are suggestive bounded instances of adaptive route exposure under veto. They are
not an exhaustive adjacent-possible census, open-ended evolution, or evidence for
an unprestatable physical phase space.

## Review boundary and evidence rule

This is a targeted critical addendum, not a systematic review or meta-analysis.
The source set was selected to include the originating definition, the strongest
unprestatability/enablement statement, the main urn and network formalizations,
combinatorial TAP models and a direct model criticism, dynamic scaffolding,
higher-order novelty, and a long-run empirical application with resource
constraints. Native results are separated from the ICP/IOTA synthesis. The
source order below is fixed to project IDs S176--S186.

## Primary source set and native results

### S176 --- Kauffman (2000), *Investigations*

- Primary source: Stuart A. Kauffman, *Investigations* (Oxford University Press,
  2000). DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780195121049.001.0001>.
- Primary locator: Chapter 7, pp. 141--157, especially p. 142; chapter DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780195121049.003.0007>.
- Native object: the ``actual'' is a reaction graph of molecular kinds and
  reactions. Its adjacent possible comprises molecular kinds absent from the
  actual but synthesizable from actual substrates in one reaction step. When a
  member becomes actual, the actual and its one-step boundary change.
- ICP relevance: this is the closest antecedent to a novelty-filtered one-step
  reachable support. It establishes that ``possible'' must be relative to a
  current actual set and to a specified notion of one step.
- Strongest boundary: the chapter supplies a conceptual and chemical program,
  not a carrier-intervention experiment. The frontier depends on what counts as
  a molecular kind, allowed reaction, resource, and step. It does not by itself
  supply a probability law, exhaustive measurement procedure, or
  representation-independent scalar.

### S177 --- Longo, Montévil, and Kauffman (2012), enablement

- Primary source: Giuseppe Longo, Maël Montévil, and Stuart A. Kauffman, ``No
  Entailing Laws, but Enablement in the Evolution of the Biosphere,'' *GECCO '12
  Companion*, pp. 1379--1392. DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.1145/2330784.2330946>; author manuscript:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.2069>.
- Primary locator: abstract and overview; discussion of changing phase spaces
  and relevant observables; Sec. 8.2, ``Causes and Enablement''; conclusion.
- Native claim: biological evolution persistently changes the relevant
  observables, niches, and phase space in ways the authors argue cannot be
  prestated. Differential physical causes remain available, but environments
  and organisms also enable later evolutionary possibilities without entailing
  a unique trajectory.
- ICP relevance: this is extremely close to ICP language about constraints that
  enable futures and about history changing the future possibility structure.
  It also warns against treating a biological niche as a fixed physical boundary
  condition.
- Strongest boundary: the unprestatability thesis is a theoretical and
  philosophical argument, not a theorem derived from a formal impossibility
  criterion or a controlled empirical result. If a study exhaustively declares
  and measures a frontier, that operational surrogate is prestatable by design.
  The strong ontic thesis therefore cannot be used simultaneously as the
  directly enumerated experimental variable.

### S178 --- Tria et al. (2014), correlated novelties

- Primary source: Francesca Tria, Vittorio Loreto, Vito D. P. Servedio, and
  Steven H. Strogatz, ``The Dynamics of Correlated Novelties,'' *Scientific
  Reports* 4, 5890. DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1038/srep05890>.
- Primary locator: Introduction; ``A simple generative model'' and Fig. 5;
  ``A model with semantics''; Supplementary Information for the asymptotics;
  Discussion.
- Native construction: in the urn model with triggering, every draw is returned
  with \(\rho\) reinforcing copies. If the drawn color is novel to the realized
  sequence, \(\nu+1\) new distinct colors are added. The urn is the currently
  available possibility space while the draw sequence is realized history.
  Reinforcement and triggering jointly generate Heaps- and Zipf-like regimes;
  semantic weighting adds correlations between nearby novelties.
- Native evidence: the study compares predicted novelty rates and triggering
  signatures with Wikipedia edits, social tags, words in texts, and Last.fm
  listening histories. It explicitly distinguishes novelty to an observer from
  innovation new to the world.
- ICP relevance: the model is a direct formal example of a realized novelty
  changing the support from which later events are drawn. It also shows that
  Zipf and Heaps patterns can arise from an explicit reinforcement-plus-triggering
  rival rather than from an ICP carrier law.
- Strongest boundary: the complete adjacent space is not observed in the data;
  the study uses indirect signatures. The generative rule inserts new colors by
  construction, and other mechanisms can also produce Heaps and Zipf laws. A
  first encounter with an already existing song or word is not ontic creation.

### S179 --- Iacopini, Milojević, and Latora (2018), innovation networks

- Primary source: Iacopo Iacopini, Staša Milojević, and Vito Latora, ``Network
  Dynamics of Innovation Processes,'' *Physical Review Letters* 120, 048301.
  DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.120.048301>.
- Primary locator: abstract; model definition and transition rule; empirical
  comparison with knowledge growth in scientific disciplines; conclusion and
  supplemental material.
- Native construction: cognition is represented as a random walk on a network
  of linked concepts; a first node visit is an innovation. Traversal reinforces
  edge weights, which in turn modify later transition probabilities, so the
  exploratory process and network coevolve. The model reproduces novelty rates
  and correlations reported in the analyzed scientific-data sets.
- ICP relevance: topology provides an explicit adjacent frontier and retained
  traversal history changes later discovery probabilities. It is a strong rival
  for generic claims that remembered paths organize later novelty.
- Strongest boundary: nodes, links, and the update law are modeled objects. A
  first visit is discovery relative to the walker, not proof that a physically
  new state became possible. Model fit does not identify a persistent carrier or
  show that a carrier intervention changes the frontier.

### S180 --- Steel, Hordijk, and Kauffman (2020), combinatorial birth--death

- Primary source: Mike Steel, Wim Hordijk, and Stuart A. Kauffman, ``Dynamics of
  a Birth--Death Process Based on Combinatorial Innovation,'' *Journal of
  Theoretical Biology* 491, 110187. DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jtbi.2020.110187>; full model text:
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.03290>.
- Primary locator: Sec. 2, especially Eqs. (3)--(7) and Theorem 1; Sec. 2.2 and
  Theorem 2; simulations and Discussion.
- Native construction: for \(n\) existing items, the combinatorial formation
  birth rate is

  \[
  \lambda_n=\sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_i {n\choose i},
  \]

  while items die at rate \(\mu n\). In the geometric case
  \(\alpha_i=P\alpha^i\), \(\lambda_n=P[(1+\alpha)^n-1]\). Under the paper's
  pure-birth conditions, the process explodes in finite random time with finite
  mean and variance. With allowed extinction and the stated nonlinear
  conditions, the process eventually reaches extinction or explosion with
  probability one.
- ICP relevance: this is an explicit signed gain/loss precedent and a formal
  rival for a rapid transition in the number of types.
- Strongest boundary: the state variable counts items and largely suppresses
  their identity, mechanism, resources, and compatibility. Finite-time
  explosion follows from the specified combinatorial rate; it is not an observed
  law of biospheres or economies. The paper itself notes that a conventional
  birth--death process with a changing birth rate can mimic the illustrative
  historical curve.

### S181 --- Ubaldi et al. (2021), social-network exploration

- Primary source: Enrico Ubaldi, Raffaella Burioni, Vittorio Loreto, and
  Francesca Tria, ``Emergence and Evolution of Social Networks through
  Exploration of the Adjacent Possible Space,'' *Communications Physics* 4, 28.
  DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1038/s42005-021-00527-1>.
- Primary locator: Introduction; Model and ``Exploration strategies''; Fig. 3;
  empirical results; Conclusion and Methods.
- Native construction: each person is represented by an urn containing IDs of
  possible contacts. Repeated contact reinforces an ID by \(\rho\) copies; a
  first contact makes a link actual and exchanges a memory buffer containing
  \(\nu+1\) IDs, thereby adding potential contacts. The model distinguishes
  actual, adjacent, and non-adjacent regions.
- Native evidence: fitted variants reproduce several local and global features
  of Twitter mentions, APS coauthorship, and mobile-phone calls, including
  exploration rates, weighted-degree patterns, clustering, and community
  structure.
- ICP relevance: the transferred memory buffer is an explicit mechanism by
  which interaction history enlarges a later contact frontier.
- Strongest boundary: possible contacts are encoded as urn IDs under one of
  several sharing strategies. The result is a generative fit to observed social
  networks, not a randomized carrier intervention. Triadic closure,
  reinforcement, homophily, and other network models remain ordinary rivals.

### S182 --- Villani and Serra (2023), explicit novelty and rediscovery

- Primary source: Marco Villani and Roberto Serra, ``Super-Exponential Growth in
  Models of a Binary String World,'' *Entropy* 25, 168. DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.3390/e25010168>.
- Primary locator: abstract; Secs. 1--2 and canonical TAP Eq. (3); Binary String
  World construction; continuous limit and simulations; Discussion.
- Native question: canonical TAP counts combinations as potential new types and
  may overcount when a combination regenerates a previously known type. The
  Binary String World makes type identity explicit through breakup and joining
  of binary strings, permitting rediscoveries to be rejected.
- Native result: its continuous approximation has finite-time divergence, and
  finite simulations remain TAP-like rather than merely exponential under the
  reported variants even after duplicate types are discarded.
- ICP relevance: this is the required adversarial check against a pure
  combinatorial-count artifact. It shows both why outcome identity must be
  retained and that some explicit-type systems still display rapid growth.
- Strongest boundary: the string grammar and novelty classifier are supplied.
  A finite simulation cannot literally diverge, and finite resources ultimately
  invalidate the approximation. Robustness within selected Binary String World
  variants does not establish a universal natural scaling law.

### S183 --- Griesemer (2024), dynamic scaffolding

- Primary source: James Griesemer, ``Modeling Scaffolded Development into the
  Adjacent Possible Environment,'' *Philosophy of Science* 91(5), 1404--1414.
  DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1017/psa.2023.144>.
- Primary locator: Sec. 2 on Kauffman's adjacent possible; the Rainey ecological
  scaffolding case; the Tria model; ``Eco-devo scaffolding in the Tria model'';
  Conclusion.
- Native contribution: the paper interprets ecological scaffolds as dynamic,
  developing systems rather than fixed boundary conditions and sketches how a
  scaffold can bias development into a changing adjacent-possible environment.
  It uses Rainey's experimental system as an empirical target for a formal
  modeling scenario.
- ICP relevance: constraints need not merely prune. A scaffold can facilitate
  and redirect development, and the scaffold itself can change. That is close to
  a carrier-mediated, cross-level enabling account.
- Strongest boundary: this is a symposia paper and modeling proposal, not a new
  experimental intervention on an adjacent frontier. It conceptually
  anticipates ``constraint-enabled novelty,'' so that phrase alone is not an ICP
  prediction.

### S184 --- Cortês et al. (2025), the TAP equation

- Primary source: Marina Cortês, Stuart A. Kauffman, Andrew R. Liddle, and Lee
  Smolin, ``The TAP Equation: Evaluating Combinatorial Innovation,'' *European
  Economic Review* 179, 105144. DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.euroecorev.2025.105144>.
- Primary locator: model definitions and analytic approximations; numerical
  validation of blow-up times; equilibrium between innovation and extinction;
  two-scale TAP model; Data Availability statement.
- Native construction: one studied form evolves the number of types as

  \[
  M_{t+1}=(1-\mu)M_t+
  \sum_{i=2}^{M_t}\alpha_i {M_t\choose i}.
  \]

  Under the analyzed parameterizations, the generic trajectory has a long
  plateau followed by sharp explosive growth. A two-scale variant replaces the
  plateau with an initial exponential phase. The authors derive approximations
  for blow-up times and compare them with simulation.
- ICP relevance: TAP is the direct existing theory for a combinatorial
  possibility-space transition. ICP cannot claim a generic plateau, hockey
  stick, or blow-up as its unique cosmological or evolutionary signature.
- Strongest boundary: the paper is mathematical and phenomenological; its Data
  Availability statement says no data were used. The transition is generated by
  the stipulated combinatorial terms and coefficients, not derived from an ICP
  carrier or independently measured law.

### S185 --- Di Bona et al. (2025), higher-order novelties

- Primary source: Gabriele Di Bona, Alessandro Bellina, Giordano De Marzo,
  Angelo Petralia, Iacopo Iacopini, and Vito Latora, ``The Dynamics of
  Higher-Order Novelties,'' *Nature Communications* 16, 393. DOI:
  <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-55115-y>.
- Primary locator: Introduction and higher-order Heaps-law definitions;
  empirical comparisons; edge-reinforced random walk with triggering model;
  Methods and code record.
- Native result: the authors count first occurrences not only of individual
  items but of pairs and higher combinations in music, text, and scientific
  concept sequences. Higher-order Heaps laws distinguish processes with similar
  first-order discovery rates. An edge-reinforced random walk with triggering
  lets first visits to nodes and first traversals of links reinforce and expand
  a coevolving network, reproducing the reported range of scaling exponents.
- ICP relevance: a frontier can change in relations among known items even when
  no new first-order item appears. ICP therefore cannot equate possibility-space
  change with a single cardinality of states.
- Strongest boundary: ``new'' remains relative to the sequence construction,
  item classifier, order, and network model. Scaling fits do not identify a
  causal physical carrier, and higher-order Heaps behavior is not unique evidence
  for ICP.

### S186 --- Taalbi (2026), long-run discovery under constraints

- Primary source: Josef Taalbi, ``Long-Run Patterns in the Discovery of the
  Adjacent Possible,'' *Industrial and Corporate Change* 35(1), 123--149.
  DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1093/icc/dtaf028> (published online 2025; issue 2026).
- Primary locator: Sec. 2, especially Eqs. (1)--(8) and resource-constrained
  hypotheses; Sec. 3 data; Fig. 4 and Tables 3--4; product-space prediction;
  Discussion.
- Native construction: organizations recombine a resource-limited active subset
  \(D^*\leq D\) of their knowledge types. Under the paper's constrained-search
  model, the long-run innovation rate becomes linear in cumulative innovations,
  corresponding to exponential rather than persistent super-exponential growth.
- Native evidence: Swedish new-product records from 1908--2016 show an initially
  superlinear but overall approximately linear dependence of innovation rate on
  cumulative innovation, a Heaps-law product-diversity exponent reported as
  \(0.587\), and short-run predictive information in product-space proximity.
  The product-space constraints weaken over long horizons.
- ICP relevance: this is the strongest empirical check in the batch. It supports
  historically conditioned and partly predictable adjacent discovery while
  directly challenging unrestricted TAP blow-up.
- Strongest boundary: the unit is an organization and products are classified
  from historical trade-journal records. Resource constraints, firm capability,
  and classifier choices are substantive model inputs. The correlations do not
  establish a physical possibility frontier, an ICP carrier, or universal
  scaling.

## Cross-source formal synthesis

### An operational adjacent frontier

The literature makes ``adjacent'' meaningful only relative to a mechanism and
grain. Let \(r_\ell:\Omega\rightarrow\mathcal K_\ell\) be a frozen map from
micro-outcomes to types or roles at scale \(\ell\). Let \(\mathcal V_t^\ell\)
be the types already actualized by time \(t\), and let
\(R_\tau^\pi(m_t)\) be the reachable support under policy \(\pi\), declared
resources and boundary, and horizon \(\tau\). Define

\[
\mathcal J_{t,\tau}^{\pi,\ell}(m_t)
=r_\ell\!\left(R_\tau^\pi(m_t)\right)\setminus\mathcal V_t^\ell.
\]

For a registered single transition, \(\mathcal J\) is an operational version of
Kauffman's adjacent possible. At longer horizons it is an accessible-novelty
set, not literally a one-step frontier. It is not observer-free: changing the
role map, policy, horizon, boundary, resource budget, tolerance, or ``actual''
repertoire can change it. This dependence is a feature to register and test,
not something a scalar can erase.

### Enabled, lost, and retained possibilities

The existing IOTA relative-entropy identity assumes nested pruning. Adjacent
possible work also requires support expansion and non-nested replacement. For
matched prior and post supports \(R_0\) and \(R_1\), retain the set ledger

\[
K=R_0\cap R_1,\qquad
E=R_1\setminus R_0,\qquad
L=R_0\setminus R_1,
\]

where \(K\), \(E\), and \(L\) are retained, enabled, and lost possibilities.
For a declared common measure \(\mu\), define the signed log-volume deformation

\[
\chi(R_1:R_0)=\log\frac{\mu(R_0)}{\mu(R_1)}.
\]

Thus \(\chi>0\) is net contraction and \(\chi<0\) is net expansion. For
nonempty finite uniform nested supports, the exact dual identity is

\[
\chi(R_1:R_0)=
\begin{cases}
D_{\mathrm{KL}}(U_{R_1}\Vert U_{R_0}), & R_1\subseteq R_0
\quad\text{(pruning)},\\[3pt]
-D_{\mathrm{KL}}(U_{R_0}\Vert U_{R_1}), & R_0\subseteq R_1
\quad\text{(enabling)}.
\end{cases}
\]

The first branch recovers the registered finite-IOTA quantity exactly:

\[
\Delta I_x
=\log\frac{|R_x^{\mathrm{prior}}|}{|R_x^{\mathrm{post}}|}
=\chi_x
=D_{\mathrm{KL}}(U_x^{\mathrm{post}}\Vert U_x^{\mathrm{prior}}).
\]

The KL direction changes because strict support creation makes the pruning
direction infinite, just as strict pruning makes its reverse infinite. For
non-nested supports, \(\chi\) is not a divergence and not a complete description:
equal-sized supports can have \(\chi=0\) despite total identity turnover. The
\((E,L,K)\) ledger must therefore accompany it; a declared common-support
quantity such as Jensen--Shannon divergence may summarize turnover without
pretending it is the nested KL identity. Empty supports require explicit set or
absorbing-outcome treatment, not hidden pseudocounts.

## Exact relationship to ICP

The adjacent-possible literature establishes four points before ICP:

1. realized history can alter which types are immediately accessible;
2. novelty can expose further novelties and create correlations in discovery;
3. constraints and scaffolds can enable as well as suppress development; and
4. combinatorial and network rules can generate transitions and scaling laws in
   a changing modeled possibility space.

Accordingly, ICP cannot claim those generic statements as new. Its possible
addition is the conjunction that the literature usually leaves optional:

1. a controlled history forms a specified physical carrier;
2. the carrier persists after the initiating event;
3. carrier ablation, transplantation, or replacement causally changes a
   preregistered future law or adjacent frontier under matched resources;
4. the enabled/lost/retained result survives reasonable role maps, scales,
   horizons, and state augmentation; and
5. the operative role can persist through succession of physical witnesses.

This is a stronger experimental burden, not evidence already supplied by the
formal resemblance. Standard complete-state dynamics remains the default null:
if a sufficiently specified physical state and update law already determine the
same frontier, ICP is a useful higher-level description unless it adds an
invariant, theorem, or held-out prediction.

There is also a principled tension in the source set. Kauffman's one-step
chemical frontier and the later urn/network/string/TAP models are formalizable
because they specify types and generators. Longo, Montévil, and Kauffman's
stronger evolutionary thesis says the relevant biological phase space itself
cannot be prestated. ICP should not slide between these claims. It can test a
declared operational frontier, or it can discuss ontic unprestatability as a
philosophical conjecture; the latter cannot be inferred from successful finite
enumeration of the former.

## Bounded interpretation of IOTA Findings 068--070

The IOTA result is genuinely close to an adaptive adjacent frontier when read at
the registered continuation-role scale:

- **Finding 068:** among 164 factual unique-child continuations selected before
  the counterfactual outcome, vetoing the realized child while retaining source
  amplitude exposed an alternate continuation in 122 branches; the other 42
  lost the lineage at the endpoint.
- **Finding 069:** applying the frozen first-next-child veto policy recursively
  produced 143 observed cut depths across the original 164 branches. Nineteen
  branches still handed off after eight vetoes and were right-censored above the
  depth limit; two were source-survival censored.
- **Finding 070:** the same policy was independently applied to 79 factual
  unique-child continuations from four newly screened source geometries. Sixty-eight
  had observed cuts, ten still handed off after eight vetoes, and one was
  source-survival censored.

These experiments show that a naturally realized successor is often not the
only later continuation and that suppressing successive realized routes can
expose an ordered, history-dependent chain of alternatives. The sequence is
adaptive rather than a census of a static reserve: each veto retains amplitude
at the source, changes the ensuing world, and selects the next realized child in
that counterfactual history. That makes the result informative for an
intervention-indexed adjacent frontier and simultaneously blocks the claim that
Finding 069 measured a fixed graph-theoretic cut or exhaustively listed all
routes present in the factual world.

The limits are decisive. The branches are conditional on factual unique-child
continuation, the policy and horizon are frozen but particular, and some depths
are censored. The simulator has a finite lattice, supplied update rule, supplied
state variables, and finite continuation role. It demonstrates bounded dynamic
accessibility within a fixed formal state space. It does **not** demonstrate
ontic creation of new physical states, an unprestatable phase space,
open-ended evolution, or a universal adjacent-possible growth law.

## Implication for the GfE bridge

The adjacent-possible extension resolves one algebraic obstruction in the
proposed IOTA--Gravity-from-Entropy bridge. GfE's local entropy-mode contribution
\(c_k=\ln\mathcal G_k\) can be positive or negative, while a KL divergence is
nonnegative. The signed support deformation can have either sign and fits the
same determinant algebra:

\[
-\ln\det_F\Theta_x
=\ln\frac{\mu(R_x^{\mathrm{prior}})}
           {\mu(R_x^{\mathrm{post}})}
=\chi_x.
\]

Nested pruning gives positive forward KL; nested enabling gives negative
reverse KL. A conditional sectorwise dictionary could therefore be written

\[
\chi_{x,k}\;\widehat{=}\;c_k,\qquad
\mathcal G_k=e^{\chi_{x,k}},\qquad
\theta_k=e^{\chi_{x,k}}-1,\qquad
\frac{\delta\epsilon_k}{\delta v}
=e^{\chi_{x,k}}-1-\chi_{x,k}.
\]

This removes only the sign mismatch. It does not construct the positive
future-law operators, prove a mode dictionary, define the common measure,
derive covariance or a continuum limit, or show that a GfE geometric mode is an
IOTA adjacent frontier. In the non-nested case \(\chi\) remains a net-volume
contrast and must be paired with \((E,L,K)\).

A potentially discriminating future result would be a prospectively registered
cross-scale sign inversion under the same carrier intervention, for example

\[
\chi_{\mathrm{micro}}>0
\quad\text{and}\quad
\chi_{\mathrm{role}}<0,
\]

meaning that local micro-paths are pruned while higher-level novel roles become
accessible. Adjacent-possible and scaffolding theories already anticipate that
constraints can enable novelty, so the qualitative sign pattern is not unique.
It becomes a genuine ICP/GfE signature only if a carrier-succession invariant or
independently derived cross-level determinant map predicts its magnitude,
transition point, or sector structure before observation and named rivals do
not.

## Scaling and observational-signature verdict

- **Heaps and Zipf laws:** established outputs of urn triggering and other
  mechanisms; neither law identifies ICP.
- **Novelty clustering:** directly modeled by semantic triggering and evolving
  networks; not unique to a persistent carrier.
- **Higher-order Heaps laws:** useful evidence that relation identity matters,
  but still classifier- and sequence-relative.
- **TAP plateau and blow-up:** already predicted by combinatorial AP models and
  sensitive to coefficients, duplicate handling, death, finite resources, and
  the type definition. It cannot be presented as a novel ICP cosmological
  transition.
- **Resource-constrained exponential growth:** supported in Taalbi's
  organizational setting and a strong rival to unrestricted blow-up; not a
  universal physical law either.
- **Cross-level signed determinant relation:** not yet observed. It is the
  sharper research bridge because it joins a controlled carrier intervention,
  support identity, scale, and GfE sign structure rather than selecting an
  already-known curve shape.

## Strongest nulls and rival explanations

- **Fixed-state-space null:** ``new'' means newly visited, newly classified, or
  newly probable within an ambient state space already supplied by the complete
  dynamics.
- **Classifier null:** frontier growth or contraction is created by changing the
  type map, novelty threshold, scale, or definition of one step.
- **Reinforcement/network null:** path dependence and correlated novelty arise
  from ordinary reinforcement, triadic closure, or a coevolving network without
  a distinct ICP carrier principle.
- **Combinatorial null:** rapid TAP growth follows from counting stipulated
  combinations; duplicate rediscovery, type death, incompatibility, and finite
  resources remove or delay it.
- **Complete-state causal null:** after adequate state augmentation, the
  apparent record is an ordinary physical state variable and ICP adds no
  prediction.
- **Selection and censoring null:** a frontier is inferred from branches selected
  because they already continued, with unobserved alternatives or unresolved
  right tails.
- **Finite-saturation null:** a bounded simulator can show adaptive exposure but
  cannot establish open-ended novelty or a continually expanding phase space.
- **GfE algebra null:** matching signed log determinants is a chosen
  representation of volume change, not a derived physical identity.

## Decisive next benchmark

A credible adjacent-frontier experiment for ICP should prospectively fix:

1. the micro-outcome space, role/type map \(r_\ell\), actual repertoire, meaning
   of one step, horizon, policies, resources, measure, and detection tolerance;
2. a history that forms a candidate carrier and a matched carrier-null history;
3. a held-out method for estimating the prior frontier before opening outcomes;
4. carrier ablation, transplantation, restoration, and sham interventions while
   matching the rest of the measured physical state;
5. complete \((E,L,K)\) reporting plus \(\chi\), probability reweighting, and a
   bounded turnover diagnostic where supports are non-nested;
6. alternative plausible classifiers, scales, policies, horizons, and augmented
   state descriptions;
7. replacement of the original witness to test carrier-role succession; and
8. preregistered comparison against fixed-state reachability, reinforcement,
   evolving-network, TAP, resource-constrained, and ordinary complete-state
   models.

For a GfE claim, the future-law operator and geometric operator must then be
constructed independently. A scale-dependent determinant agreement, sector
transition, or cross-level sign inversion must be predicted without fitting the
identity and must outperform GR/\(\Lambda\)CDM, native GfE, and non-gravitational
branching/percolation rivals.

## Batch verdict

**Supported as established prior art:** realized novelty can condition later
accessibility; formal expanding-space models generate correlated novelties and
several scaling regimes; network topology, resource limits, and dynamic
scaffolds shape discovery; explicit combinatorial models can exhibit sharp
growth transitions.

**Supported within bounded IOTA project evidence:** controlled vetoes expose
history-dependent alternate continuation roles and heterogeneous ordered cut
