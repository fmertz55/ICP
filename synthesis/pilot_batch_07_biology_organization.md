# Biological organization closure for ICP

## Bounded adversarial literature report

**Search cutoff:** 2026-07-20  
**Scope:** biological constraint closure; physically embodied heredity and memory; evolutionary path accessibility; autocatalysis; synthetic compartments and synthetic cells; and the strongest redescription/null critiques.  
**Review type:** structured critical synthesis, not a systematic review or meta-analysis.  
**Evidence rule:** peer-reviewed primary experiments and original theoretical work were preferred. One exceptionally current preprint is quarantined as frontier evidence and is not used to support the verdict. Reviews, press accounts, and nontechnical summaries were excluded from the evidentiary core.  
**Stopping rule:** stop when each target mechanism has at least one direct intervention or constructive result, the strongest known null has been represented, and further searching is yielding refinements rather than a different conclusion.

## Outcome first

Biology gives ICP **real but bounded legs as an operational research program**. It does not establish ICP as a fundamental physical principle.

The strongest biologically supported core is:

> A prior interaction can construct a physically embodied record that persists after the initiating event and, under controlled perturbation, causally changes the distribution—and sometimes the operationally accessible set—of later responses.

This statement is supported across distinct substrates:

- acquired CRISPR spacers change later phage susceptibility, and spacer addition/removal changes resistance specificity;
- a chromatin state created by earlier inositol starvation persists for several mitotic divisions after removal of an initiating transcription factor, while intervention on the writer/reader machinery abolishes persistence and faster reactivation;
- historical substitutions alter which later protein mutations are selectively accessible;
- lipid boundaries suppress parasitic replicators and preserve full-length replicators relative to a matched bulk environment;
- engineered autocatalytic and compartmentalized replicators can maintain, diversify, and select molecular functions under deliberately supplied reaction conditions.

Those findings support **embodied persistence and causal future-shaping**. They do **not** show that information persists without a physical carrier, that information is ontologically prior to matter and energy, that the laws plus a fully specified physical state are insufficient, or that mass/energy emerge from an information lattice.

The broad idea is also not novel. Kolchinsky and Wolpert already define semantic information by counterfactually scrambling system–environment correlations and measuring effects on viability. More directly, Sowinski and colleagues' 2025 **Causal Leverage Density** (CLD) measures the effect of information-scrambling interventions on distributions of future phase-space trajectories. If ICP is stated only as “information changes possible futures,” CLD and adjacent causal/semantic-information work substantially anticipate it. ICP's defensible opportunity is therefore a narrower conjunction:

1. a specified history constructs a record;
2. that record remains physically embodied after the initiating cause is gone;
3. intervention on the record, with other relevant state and resources matched, changes a defined future distribution or reachable support;
4. the conclusion is robust to reasonable choices of boundary, coarse-graining, horizon, and intervention family; and
5. an IOTA model eventually constructs such records and their effects rather than merely redescribing them.

That conjunction is scientifically useful and testable. The present biological literature supplies strong examples of items 1–3, partial guidance for item 4, and no evidence for item 5.

## What “constraint” means in the relevant biology

Montévil and Mossio's closure-of-constraints framework distinguishes processes that transform material from constraints that act on those processes while remaining approximately conserved on the timescale of interest. A biological organization is closed, in their technical sense, when its constraints depend on and help maintain one another, even while matter and energy flow through the thermodynamically open system. This is a useful model of enzymes, membranes, genetic templates, and regulatory structures.

Three cautions matter for ICP:

1. **Constraint status is relational and scale-dependent.** An enzyme can be conserved relative to one catalytic event but synthesized and degraded on another timescale. The analyst must specify the system boundary, process, and horizon.
2. **Closure is not empirical proof of a new ontology.** Garson's liberality objection argues that self-maintenance criteria may classify too many accidental or nonfunctional structures. Corti shows that factual counterfactual conditions for continued existence do not, by themselves, entail normativity or what a component “ought” to do. Rider's model-theoretic defense is useful precisely because it treats closure as a strategic modeling framework that guides empirical work, not as an analytic definition of life.
3. **The 2026 debate narrows the claim further.** Cuciniello argues that a minimal closure model can naturalize the teleology of physiological self-maintenance, but that broader developmental or regulative teleology remains theoretically difficult. ICP should therefore use closure as a disciplined model of organized dependence, not as evidence that all future-directed biological behavior has one cause.

## The strongest examples, read adversarially

### 1. A stored history can be causally edited: CRISPR

Barrangou and colleagues exposed *Streptococcus thermophilus* to bacteriophages and found acquisition of phage-derived CRISPR spacers in resistant descendants. Adding or deleting spacers altered resistance. This is unusually clean for ICP because the past encounter creates an identifiable sequence record and experimental editing of that record changes a later challenge response.

The disciplined inference is not “information acts separately from matter.” It is that a sequence-bearing molecular structure, constructed by prior exposure, is a causal state variable for a later intervention. The standard biological account—base pairing, CRISPR expression, and molecular targeting—is sufficient. ICP earns value only if it generalizes and quantifies the history–carrier–future relation more effectively than that account.

### 2. A record can outlast its initiator, but not all physical realization: INO1 memory

Yeast INO1 transcriptional memory is the best bounded example of the “persisted information” intuition. Earlier work identified an MRS DNA element, nuclear-pore interaction, H2A.Z, and other components required for memory and faster reactivation. Sump and colleagues then separated establishment from inheritance with inducible degradation. If Sfl1 was removed before memory formed, memory-associated H3K4me2 did not form. When Sfl1 was removed after establishment, H3K4me2 persisted for roughly four generations. Removing Set3, part of the reader/maintenance system, caused rapid loss; the mark also depends on COMPASS writer activity.

Thus the record becomes partly independent of the event and factor that initiated it, while remaining dependent on a continuing material maintenance network. This is exactly the distinction ICP should preserve:

- **supported:** information about an earlier condition can persist separately from the initiating condition and from a particular initiator;
- **unsupported:** information can persist separately from every physical carrier, copying process, or energetic cost.

The same experiment also shows why an instantaneous state description must be specified carefully. A state description that omits chromatin marks and maintenance complexes will appear causally incomplete; a sufficiently detailed physical state already includes them.

### 3. History changes evolutionary accessibility, but “possible” needs a model

Weinreich and colleagues constructed all 32 combinations of five substitutions associated with cefotaxime resistance in TEM beta-lactamase. Under their measured landscape and a strong-selection/weak-mutation, monotonically improving-path assumption, only 18 of 120 direct paths were selectively accessible. Harms and Thornton showed a deeper version: ancestral glucocorticoid-receptor evolution depended on permissive substitutions that had little direct functional effect but made later function-changing substitutions physically tolerable; alternatives were rare because of protein-stability constraints.

These studies strongly support the claim that inherited context changes which next steps are likely or operationally accessible. They do not license a context-free statement that other futures are physically impossible. Accessibility changes with population size, drift, recombination, mutation spectrum, environmental schedule, phenotype definition, and time horizon. ICP should therefore distinguish:

- **probability reweighting:** the record changes probabilities over future trajectories;
- **operational inaccessibility:** no path is observed or allowed under specified interventions, thresholds, and horizon;
- **physical impossibility:** excluded by the full dynamics, a much stronger claim rarely established by biological experiments.

### 4. Autocatalysis can organize replication, but is not sufficient for heredity

Lincoln and Joyce constructed two RNA enzymes that catalyze each other's synthesis, producing exponential amplification and heritable recombinant variants under serial transfer. Vaidya and colleagues showed that a cooperative three-member RNA network outgrew selfish autocatalytic cycles. These are constructive demonstrations: local catalytic relations can close into a network that changes which molecular populations persist.

Vasas and colleagues provide the necessary null. In tested GARD compositional-assembly models, homeostatic growth and compositional self-reproduction did not yield selected evolvability. The future composition largely reflected an imposed kinetic interaction matrix. The general lesson is robust even if that model is not decisive for all protocells: **autocatalysis, self-maintenance, and apparent inheritance cannot be treated as interchangeable**. ICP should demand interventions that separate templated heredity, compositional relaxation, selection, and externally imposed cycling.

### 5. Compartments genuinely change trajectories—and external scaffolding still matters

Compartmentalization studies increasingly approach the required intervention. Ichihashi and colleagues evolved a translation-coupled RNA replicator for hundreds of generations; the compartmentalized system improved while a corresponding bulk/no-compartment condition did not show the same response. Mizuuchi and Ichihashi showed that cooperative RNAs could persist in droplets despite parasites, and later work tracked diversification into interacting host and parasite lineages across 240 rounds. Abil and colleagues made the boundary comparison especially direct: a synthetic DNA self-replicator retained its full-length form in liposomes, whereas shorter parasitic templates dominated bulk reactions.

These results establish that a boundary is not just a visual container. It changes encounter rates, co-inheritance, resource sharing, and the competition between full-length and parasitic replicators. But the experimental cycle still includes externally supplied PURE components, fresh substrate, fusion or freeze–thaw manipulation, dilution, and/or imposed droplet transfer. Those operations are part of the causal architecture and cannot be hidden outside the declared system boundary.

The 2026 state of the art sharpens this boundary:

- Restrepo Sierra and colleagues integrated a roughly 9.6-kb self-replicating DNA program with phospholipid synthesis inside liposomes. In an initial high-content assay, about 8% of vesicles showed both activities; the full genome amplified about tenfold versus about hundredfold for a shorter replicator, and the reported lipid yield was not sufficient for detectable vesicle growth. Cleaner template preparation raised the fully active fraction in a later assay, but the system still depends on supplied PURE machinery, substrates, and energy.
- Sierra Heras and Danelon linked selectable gene functions to DNA self-replication through engineered positive-feedback couplings. This is a useful direct demonstration that a molecular function can alter the future copy-number distribution of its carrier. It remains a designed in-vitro selection architecture.
- Khanal and colleagues generated lipid bilayers de novo from simple metabolites using soluble enzymes, with pores enabling continued precursor entry and glycolipid compartments maintaining proton gradients. This relaxes the assumption that every membrane must descend from a membrane, but it does not yet couple the boundary to genome heredity and self-reproduction.
- The July 2026 “SpudCell” bioRxiv preprint reports a larger, multipart genome and five cell-cycle-like rounds. Its feeder liposomes contain the full reaction mix, and each division is mechanically imposed by extrusion. The result is promising engineering, not yet peer-reviewed evidence of autonomous life.

Taken together, synthetic-cell research supports a graded ladder rather than a threshold announcement: **module activity → coupled modules → internally produced constraints → recurrent heredity → autonomous resource acquisition and division → open-ended evolution**. Current systems occupy impressive intermediate rungs. None closes the entire ladder.

## Closest formal prior art: a novelty constraint for ICP

### Kolchinsky–Wolpert semantic information

Kolchinsky and Wolpert define semantic information as correlations between a system and environment that are causally necessary for maintaining the system's viability. Their operation counterfactually scrambles correlations while tracking a viability functional, often related to negative Shannon entropy. This already combines information, intervention, and a future-dependent consequence.

Its limitations also identify legitimate ICP work. Results depend on the chosen system/environment partition, time horizon, viability functional, and allowed scrambling operations. The framework is not specifically about records constructed by a history, nor about a carrier that persists after its initiating cause.

### Causal Leverage Density

Sowinski and colleagues generalize beyond viability. CLD proposes scrambling information-bearing correlations and measuring the divergence between the resulting distributions of future phase-space trajectories. That is very close to the proposed ICP object

\[
H_t \rightarrow M_t \rightarrow \{P^{\pi}(F_{t:t+\tau}\mid M_t):\pi\in\Pi\}.
\]

The proximity should be stated, not buried. A generic claim that “information has meaning when erasing it changes future trajectories” is already present. The current CLD publication is chiefly a framework/proposal and does not yet provide broad empirical validation. It also does not require that the information-bearing structure was built by a prior history, persists beyond its initiating cause, or changes reachable support rather than only probability divergence.

Accordingly, ICP's biology program should either:

1. formally reduce to and extend CLD by adding history formation, embodied persistence, support-sensitive reachability, and robustness criteria; or
2. demonstrate a mathematical or empirical distinction from CLD under a shared benchmark.

Without that comparison, a reviewer can reasonably classify ICP as redescription.

## Default null and strongest redescription critique

The default null is:

> Given the laws/dynamics, a sufficiently specified physical state, boundary conditions, and intervention, the future distribution is determined in the ordinary stochastic or quantum sense. A “record” is a coarse-grained physical variable useful for prediction and intervention, not an additional substrate or causal ingredient.

Every biological example in this report is compatible with that null. CRISPR spacers are DNA; epigenetic marks are molecular modifications maintained by enzymes; evolutionary accessibility is genotype-by-environment-dependent fitness and population dynamics; compartment effects follow diffusion, sequestration, and selection; autocatalysis follows reaction kinetics.

This does not make ICP empty. Many scientifically powerful principles—selection, computation, memory, control, and thermodynamic constraints—operate at higher levels without violating microphysics. But it changes the burden of proof. ICP must deliver at least one of the following:

- a new invariant or bound not already obtained from causal modeling, control theory, semantic information, statistical mechanics, or evolutionary dynamics;
- a quantitatively superior prediction under held-out interventions;
- a principled carrier/sufficient-statistic discovery method;
- a robustness theorem showing that the effect survives reasonable repartitioning and coarse-graining;
- or an IOTA construction that yields known physical/biological organization with independently testable deviations.

Merely renaming DNA, chromatin, a membrane, or a genotype as an “information constraint” does not satisfy the burden.

## A decisive biological benchmark for ICP

A credible next experiment should force ICP to outperform the redescription null. A practical benchmark can combine the logic already proven in CRISPR and INO1 memory:

1. **History:** expose otherwise matched cells to a defined stimulus, such as a phage or inositol starvation.
2. **Record:** identify and quantify the candidate carrier after the initiating stimulus and initiating factor are gone.
3. **Carrier intervention:** erase, edit, transplant, or restore the carrier while matching transcript levels, proteins, energetic state, environment, and as much of the remaining physical state as feasible.
4. **Intervention family:** challenge cells across several future environments rather than one favored endpoint.
5. **Future object:** estimate full distributions over survival, lag time, expression trajectory, lineage persistence, and failure—not only mean fitness.
6. **Reachability test:** distinguish altered probability from loss/gain of empirical support at predeclared detection thresholds and horizons.
7. **Robustness:** repeat under alternative coarse-grainings, horizons, and system boundaries.
8. **Model comparison:** compare ordinary mechanistic state models, Kolchinsky–Wolpert semantic information, CLD, and the specified ICP metric on held-out interventions.

If ICP adds no predictive or interventional performance after the carrier and ordinary state variables are supplied, it is a useful synthesis vocabulary but not a new scientific principle. If it identifies a stable carrier statistic or reachability bound that generalizes across systems and interventions, it has earned a stronger claim.

## Viability verdict for the biology component

| Question | Verdict | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Can past interaction build a persistent physical record? | **Yes—strongly supported.** | CRISPR and chromatin-memory interventions establish it directly. |
| Can that record causally alter later response distributions? | **Yes—strongly supported.** | Spacer editing, memory-component perturbation, and genotype construction alter later outcomes. |
| Can constraints change operational path accessibility? | **Yes, conditionally.** | Fitness landscapes and compartments restrict paths under specified models, environments, and horizons. |
| Can molecular constraints build quasiparticle-like or higher-order organized entities? | **Partial constructive analogy only.** | Autocatalytic networks and synthetic compartments show organized molecular collectives, not physical quasiparticles in the field-theoretic sense. |
| Does organization close autonomously in present synthetic cells? | **Not yet.** | Major components are externally supplied and cycling/division is commonly imposed. |
| Is broad future-shaping by information novel to ICP? | **No.** | Kolchinsky–Wolpert and especially CLD are close prior art. |
| Does biology establish information as the primary substrate of mass and energy? | **No.** | All cited effects have sufficient material/mechanistic accounts and do not test IOTA ontology. |
| Is ICP scientifically viable? | **Yes, as a bounded operational and constructive hypothesis; unestablished as a universal principle.** | The history–embodied-record–intervention architecture is empirical, but novelty and added value must be demonstrated against existing frameworks. |
